## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

- 🪖 **Pentagon signed AI deployment deals with 8 companies** (AWS, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, SpaceX, NVIDIA, Reflection, Oracle) for classified IL6/IL7 networks via GenAI.mil. Anthropic is conspicuously absent after refusing autonomous weapons use — Pentagon responded by designating them a "supply chain risk." This is a defining fork in enterprise AI positioning: safety-first vs. defense-first.

- 🤫 **Anthropic quietly released "Mythos"** — its most powerful model yet — to a limited partner group, citing significant cybersecurity applications and misuse risks. Anthropic's run-rate revenue has crossed $30B (up from ~$9B at end of 2025). Claude Opus 4.7 leads GPT-5.5 on 6 of 10 shared benchmarks. Directly relevant if you're building on Claude APIs — new capability tier is coming.

- 🏗️ **Google rebranded Vertex AI to "Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform"** at Cloud Next 2026, with A2A protocol v1.0 now in production at 150 orgs. Workspace Studio (no-code agent builder) announced. Model Garden now includes 200+ models including Anthropic Claude. Google is consolidating fast against OpenAI for enterprise.

- 📋 **EU AI Act high-risk compliance deadline: August 2, 2026** — fines up to €15M or 3% of global revenue. EU is reportedly considering pushing key deadlines to 2027–2028. Colorado's SB 205 state AI law enforcement is now June 30, 2026. US federal preemption of state AI laws is in active litigation. Enterprise AI vendors need a compliance roadmap now.

- 💻 **TSMC investing $56B to scale AI chip production** amid persistent supply shortages. AMD is outperforming Nvidia post-Big Tech earnings as its GPU market share hits an inflection. Huawei's Ascend 950PR is on track for $12B revenue in 2026 (+60% YoY) — the parallel China AI compute stack is not a rounding error.

- 🤖 **Multi-agent systems are failing in production — but not for tech reasons.** Research shows the core failure mode is organizational: agents ignore each other's outputs, redo completed work, fail to delegate, and freeze in planning loops. Orchestration and control are the defining 2026 enterprise challenge, not raw model capability. Build for reliability, not just performance.

- 🧪 **Wildcard: Human scientists still outperform AI agents on complex research tasks** (Nature). After years of AI-in-science hype, empirical performance data shows AI helps at the margins but doesn't yet replace expert judgment on hard, open-ended problems. Important calibration for anyone building autonomous research or analysis pipelines.

## Sleeper Story
Colorado's SB 205 — the first comprehensive US state law governing high-risk AI systems — just had its enforcement date pushed to June 30, 2026. It's still coming. With Trump's federal preemption order also in play, the next 60 days could produce the defining US AI regulatory battle of 2026, setting precedent that every enterprise AI vendor will have to navigate.
