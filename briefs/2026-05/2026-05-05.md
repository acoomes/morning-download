# Morning Download — May 5, 2026

## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

- 🏦 **Fed holds at 3.5–3.75%; Powell's final FOMC meeting sees four dissents — most since 1992.** April 29 was Powell's last meeting as chair; his term expires mid-May. He confirmed he won't resign but will step down from the chair role, staying on the board. Four members broke from consensus (three hawkish, one dovish), a rare crack in Fed solidarity. Whoever the White House names next inherits a rate-hold standoff, an oil-driven inflation problem, and a market still watching for a blink.

- ⚡ **Middle East oil shock: oil exports down 1.5M bbl/day in Q2; parts of Asia within days of fuel-out; Europe has ~6 weeks of jet fuel.** The US–Israel strikes on Iran are cascading into a genuine global energy supply crisis. Pakistan, Indonesia, and the Philippines are the most acute cases. This is why core PCE won't hit 2%: the Fed can't cut its way out of a barrel shortage.

- 📊 **Q1 2026 GDP first read lands today after Q4 was revised down to 0.5%; major tech earnings tonight.** Private payroll growth surged Q1 to 2.5× the 2025 monthly average — labor is fine, but the economy's top-line is decelerating. Tonight's marquee earnings will test whether AI capex is being matched by revenue at sufficient speed; it's the only question anyone on a call will actually care about.

- 🪖 **Trump pulls 5,000 troops from Germany (~14% reduction), reversing the Biden post-Ukraine buildup.** Signals continued NATO retrenchment; German defense minister called it "strategically premature." Europe is quietly accelerating its own defense spending in response, which is a massive fiscal story flying below the radar.

- 💰 **AI M&A hits record pace: $10B+ deal surge, $55B EA take-private (largest sponsor-led ever), OpenAI $110B round, Anthropic $30B.** The AI premium is now the single largest valuation driver across every sector. SpaceX's acquisition of xAI adds Grok to Musk's consolidated empire. Traditional PE playbooks have been rewritten around AI capability acquisition.

- 🚀 **Wildcard: Vast Space's Haven-1 commercial space station targets May 2026 launch on SpaceX Falcon 9.** Bus-sized habitat, four-crew, 10-day missions — paying customers conduct microgravity R&D (drug testing, plant growth). First real post-ISS private space infrastructure, and it's launching this month. Almost nobody is watching.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

- 🏛️ **CISA, NSA, and international partners issue first formal guidance on deploying agentic AI systems.** Framing is blunt: agents are "software workers that can act, spend, access data, and create real business risk if given too much freedom too fast." This is the opening salvo of agentic AI governance — expect procurement and compliance requirements to follow within quarters, not years. Directly relevant to anyone building or selling enterprise agents.

- 🧠 **Anthropic releases Claude Opus 4.7 — most powerful generally available model, better at software engineering and real-world task completion.** The "Mythos Preview" still sits above it, but Opus 4.7 is the production-ready ceiling for enterprise deployments today. Positioned explicitly as less broadly capable than Mythos to manage risk surface area. The GA/preview split is becoming Anthropic's deliberate product architecture.

- 🤖 **OpenAI ships GPT-5.5 (April 23) — better coding, computer use, and deep research; rolling to paid subscribers in ChatGPT and Codex.** Less than two months after GPT-5.4. The release cadence has compressed to ~7-week cycles. If you're building product differentiation on "we use the latest model," that moat has a half-life of about six weeks.

- 🌐 **Google launches Gemini 3.1 Ultra (largest context window yet) plus a suite of enterprise agent-building tools.** Explicitly positioned to challenge OpenAI and Anthropic. Google is treating the agentic layer as existential and is moving fast. The three-way arms race (Anthropic / OpenAI / Google) is now primarily being fought at the agent orchestration layer, not the base model layer.

- 🔌 **OpenAI deprecates its Assistants API in favor of MCP with a mid-2026 sunset; MCP now at 97M installs and 10,000+ public servers.** The protocol war is over — MCP won. OpenAI surrendering its proprietary connector model is the biggest developer ecosystem signal of the month. If you have integrations built on the Assistants API, start migration planning now. If you're building new, MCP is the only rational default.

- 🖥️ **HUMAIN ONE (on AWS) launches as an enterprise operating system for building, deploying, and governing autonomous AI agents at scale.** The agentic orchestration layer is becoming a distinct product category, not a feature. AWS entering here directly is validation — and a competitive threat to everyone selling agent middleware.

- 💎 **TSMC's first Blackwell wafer rolls off US soil in Arizona; 2nm pipeline progressing; NVIDIA guides $78B Q1 FY27 revenue (+14.5% sequentially).** The US fab onshoring story is no longer hypothetical — silicon is being made in Arizona. NVIDIA's revenue trajectory continues to suggest the AI infrastructure buildout is still accelerating, not plateauing. Capacity through 2028 remains constrained.

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** Jerome Powell exits as Fed chair mid-May with four dissents at his final meeting and unresolved White House pressure on the institution. His successor inherits a rate-hold standoff, an energy-driven inflation problem, and a GDP that may be contracting — with a midterm election backdrop. The succession story is the most under-covered systemic risk in markets right now. Watch for the White House nomination; it could move bond markets more than any data print this quarter.

**One-Liner:** The old scaffolding — Fed independence, NATO troop commitments, proprietary AI protocols — is being replaced piece by piece, and today's news is just another handful of bolts on the floor.
