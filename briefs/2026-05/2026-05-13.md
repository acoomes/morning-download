## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

- 🪖 **U.S.-Iran War Cost Revised to $29B, Oil Stays Above $100** — The Pentagon raised its war cost estimate from $25B in just two weeks; energy disruption is keeping gas prices elevated and oil futures forecast above $100/barrel for weeks. Direct inflation risk: this is feeding the hot PPI print below.

- 🤝 **Trump Lands in Beijing for First Presidential China Visit Since 2017** — Trade dominates the agenda, with Trump pushing a "Board of Trade" mechanism to manage differences; Iran war costs are also on the table. Why it matters: tariff trajectory and chip export policy could both shift in 72 hours — watch this one closely.

- 📈 **PPI Jumps 1.4% in April — Hottest Monthly Print Since March 2022** — Wholesale inflation running at 6% annually, versus the 4.9% consensus estimate. The Fed has zero room to cut; any remaining hopes for H1 rate relief should be quietly shelved.

- 🏭 **Apple Seals Intel Manufacturing Deal, Intel Stock Surges 14%** — Apple agreed to use Intel and Samsung to produce chips for U.S.-bound devices, driven by reshoring pressure. The clearest signal yet that domestic semiconductor supply chains are being rebuilt under political pressure — and that Intel's revival thesis is real.

- 🇬🇧 **UK PM Keir Starmer Hemorrhaging Ministers** — Four junior ministers resigned in one week following electoral setbacks, with calls mounting for his resignation. If Starmer falls, expect a harder line on tech and AI regulation out of London.

- 💊 **Wildcard: FDA Chief Marty Makary Resigns** — The pro-deregulation head of the FDA is out after less than two years. His departure could slow the unusually brisk approval pace in biotech and digital health his tenure enabled — watch for near-term ripple effects in medtech.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

- 🚀 **Jensen Huang Crashes Trump's China Delegation at the Last Minute** — Trump personally called Huang after the NVIDIA CEO was initially excluded; Huang flew to Alaska to board Air Force One. The objective: unlock H200 chip sales to China, where Nvidia's market share collapsed from 95% to near-zero after export controls tightened. This is the most consequential AI story of the day — if export controls loosen, Nvidia's growth picture changes dramatically.

- 🏢 **Microsoft Agent 365 Now Generally Available** — Microsoft's unified control plane for observing, governing, and securing AI agents across its ecosystem is live for commercial customers. **Directly relevant to agentic platform builders**: this is the governance and compliance layer enterprise customers will expect you to integrate against.

- ☁️ **Google Launches Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform at Cloud Next 2026** — Full-stack infrastructure to build, scale, govern, and optimize agents with enterprise-grade security. Google is in a flat-out sprint to make Gemini the default agent substrate before OpenAI and Microsoft lock in the market.

- 🧠 **Anthropic Introduces "Dreaming" Technique for Autonomous Agents + Revenue Hits $30B Run Rate** — Dreaming lets agents review prior behavior to identify patterns, targeting long-running workflows in coding, finance, and law. Meanwhile, run-rate revenue surpassed $30B (up from $9B at end of 2025) — the fastest revenue ramp in enterprise software history.

- 🔐 **Google Thwarts AI-Powered Mass Exploitation Cyber Operation** — Threat Intelligence Group blocked a hacker group using AI models to plan coordinated zero-day vulnerability exploitation. First major public instance of AI as offensive cyber planning infrastructure — this is a preview of the threat landscape, not an anomaly.

- ⚖️ **EU Streamlines AI Act Compliance Rules** — Council and Parliament struck a provisional deal to simplify and reduce compliance burden under the AI Act. Directionally good for enterprise AI deployment in Europe; details on which high-risk categories are affected will matter.

- 📊 **Wall Street's Chip Darlings Rotate: Intel & AMD Now Outpacing Nvidia in 2026** — Intel and AMD have more than doubled year-to-date; Nvidia is up only 15%. The structural read: Nvidia's China export ban created a growth gap, while Intel (Apple deal) and AMD (Meta's $60B GPU commitment) are diversifying and winning new anchor customers.

## Sleeper Story

Anthropic's new legal-focused Claude plugins — connecting to Thomson Reuters CoCounsel and covering contract, employment, and litigation law — are a quiet but significant enterprise wedge. Legal is one of the highest-margin, most document-dense verticals. If these gain traction, it becomes a template for vertical-specific agentic expansion that will accelerate consolidation across legal tech incumbents (Thomson Reuters, LexisNexis, Relativity). Watch adoption metrics in Q2 earnings calls.
