# Morning Download — May 26, 2026

---

## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

- 🏦 **Kevin Warsh sworn in as Fed Chair; Trump signals rate cuts incoming** — Warsh took the oath on May 22 in a White House East Room ceremony (unusual venue, unusual chairman), confirmed by the Senate and immediately named FOMC chair unanimously. Trump explicitly tied the nomination to his desire for lower rates. This is the most consequential shift in US monetary policy governance in decades — watch for dovish signals at the June meeting and a potential re-rating of the yield curve.

- 📈 **S&P 500 posts 8th straight weekly gain; record 7,473 ahead of post-Memorial Day open** — Polymarket traders priced a 91% chance of a higher open today as Iran diplomacy calmed oil markets. Q1 2026 S&P 500 net profit margins hit 13.4% — the highest since FactSet began tracking. The rally is real and earnings-backed, not just vibes, though the 10-year yield creeping up at 4.6% is the background risk nobody wants to talk about.

- 🛢️ **Trump says Iran talks "proceeding nicely"; oil drops 5%+** — West Texas Intermediate fell sharply on optimism that the three-month-old US-Iran conflict might resolve diplomatically, with talks proceeding via backchannels. Europe remains stuck with elevated energy prices through at least 2027 regardless of outcome. A durable peace deal would be the single biggest macro positive of the year — but this has been on/off for months, so treat the rally with appropriate skepticism.

- ☢️ **Russia strikes Kyiv with Oreshnik hypersonic missile; 2 dead, 77 injured** — The Oreshnik is a nuclear-capable ballistic missile with no current intercept capability. Using it in a conventional strike on a civilian city is a deliberate escalation signal, not an operational necessity. This is the story getting buried under Iran optimism that deserves more attention.

- 🇪🇺 **EU AI Act high-risk deadline stands at August 2026 after amendment talks stall** — Negotiations to push the compliance deadline to December 2027 have collapsed. Any enterprise selling AI into European markets for healthcare, critical infrastructure, hiring, credit, or law enforcement must be compliant in ~10 weeks. Most aren't. This is an enforcement cliff that will hit enterprise software vendors hard.

- 🇨🇺 **WILDCARD: DOJ indicts Raúl Castro; Trump administration raises military intervention in Cuba** — The administration secured criminal charges against Cuba's former president the same week it raised the specter of military action. Sandwiched between Iran and Ukraine, this story is getting almost no airtime — which is exactly when these situations tend to escalate.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

- 💰 **Anthropic closes $30B funding round at $900B+ valuation — surpasses OpenAI** — Anthropic's valuation now exceeds OpenAI's $852B as the company projects $10.9B in Q2 revenue and its first-ever quarterly operating profit. Run-rate revenue is $30B+, up from $9B at year-end 2025 — that's tripling in five months. The 3.5GW compute deal with Google and Broadcom (TPUs coming online 2027) means Anthropic is building infrastructure independence from NVIDIA. This is not a research lab anymore.

- 📝 **OpenAI filing confidential S-1 with SEC; IPO window Q4 2026/early 2027** — With the Musk lawsuit dismissed and financials stabilizing, OpenAI is targeting a public debut within months. Two AI mega-IPOs (OpenAI + SpaceX/xAI, both signaled this month) hitting public markets near-simultaneously will be a gravity event for how the rest of the sector gets valued.

- 🤖 **Fujitsu unveils self-evolving multi-agent system that learns from real-world operations** — In collaboration with Carnegie Mellon University, Fujitsu's new architecture lets agent teams continuously update from execution failures, human feedback, and policy changes — no manual retraining required. Demonstrated a 28-point accuracy gain on Fujitsu's Takane LLM. The "self-evolving" pattern (agents that adapt their own behavior from operational data) is the architecture gap most enterprise platforms are trying to close right now. Worth reading the technical details.

- 🔮 **Google launches Gemini 3.5 Flash (GA), Omni world model, and Gemini Spark agent** — Flash goes generally available at 4x the speed of comparable models, priced at $1.50/$9 per 1M tokens with a 1M context window. Gemini Spark is a new general-purpose agent that reasons across connected apps (Google Workspace, etc.), in beta for AI Ultra subscribers. Omni is a new world-simulation model. Google is attacking frontier intelligence, speed/cost, and agentic UX simultaneously — this is a coordinated multi-front push.

- 💻 **TSMC 2nm ramping H2 2026; HPC now 61% of revenue as AI infra spend hits $660B** — TSMC's Q1 revenue was $35.9B (+39% YoY), with gross margin expanding to 66.2%. AI/HPC's share of revenue jumped from 46% to 61% in two years. Nvidia has reserved the majority of TSMC's most advanced packaging capacity; AMD MI450 on the same 2nm node is the next horse in the race. The compute infrastructure buildout is accelerating, not plateauing.

- 🔐 **88% of enterprises hit by AI security incidents; only 22% treat agents as identity-bearing** — Databricks' State of AI Agents report surfaces a massive and growing governance gap: most organizations are deploying agents that can access systems, execute actions, and handle sensitive data without any formal access control or identity management. As agentic deployments scale past the pilot phase, this will become the compliance story of H2 2026. If you're building agentic platforms, agent identity management is table stakes.

- 🌐 **Microsoft and xAI grant regulators early model access; Anthropic holds out on Mythos** — Major AI companies are moving toward pre-release regulatory testing in the EU and US. Anthropic has declined to provide early access to its Mythos model. EU AI Act high-risk deadlines (above) make this a collision course. The companies that navigate regulatory pre-clearance well will have a structural advantage in enterprise deals where compliance is a procurement requirement.

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** The EU AI Act high-risk compliance deadline is 10 weeks away (August 2026) and amendment talks to push it to December 2027 have officially collapsed. Enterprise software companies selling AI into healthcare, HR, finance, and critical infrastructure in Europe are about to face real enforcement exposure. Most enterprise AI buyers don't know this deadline is live — but their legal teams are about to find out. For anyone building or selling agentic platforms into European markets, this is the 30-day story with the most direct operational impact.

**One-Liner:** Anthropic quietly became the most valuable private AI company on Earth over Memorial Day weekend — the valuation inversion with OpenAI happened while the market was closed, and when it reopens, the landscape looks different.
