## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

- 🔐 **Anthropic deploys MCP tunnels + self-hosted sandboxes in Claude Managed Agents** — "MCP tunnels" lets Claude agents call internal company MCP servers via an outbound-only encrypted gateway — no inbound firewall ports required. Self-hosted sandboxes are now in public beta. *This directly unblocks enterprise agentic deployments where security teams have blocked inbound connections to private infrastructure. This is a real practical unlock for builders deploying agents inside the enterprise perimeter.*

- 🌐 **Google launches Gemini 3.5 Flash (1/3 the price), Omni world model, and Gemini Spark agent** — Flash undercuts OpenAI's comparable models on cost while maintaining frontier capability. Omni is a physical-world simulation model (significant for robotics and industrial AI). Gemini Spark is a general-purpose agent embedded in the Gemini app with cross-app reasoning. *Google is pricing aggressively and expanding the definition of "model" — they're not losing this race quietly.*

- 🤖 **Salesforce Agentforce Coworker goes beta; Camunda ProcessOS launches for agentic workflow discovery** — Salesforce embedded an AI teammate directly into CRM search surfaces (May 22). Camunda's ProcessOS (May 20) auto-discovers business processes and converts them into agentic workflows. *The enterprise workflow automation wedge is sharpening — execution agents are replacing recommendation agents as the product category.*

- 💵 **FIS + Anthropic ship Financial Crimes AI Agent for AML at BMO and Amalgamated Bank** — Anti-money-laundering investigations that took days now complete in minutes. First regulated-industry deployment of this type at scale. *The AML use case is the Trojan horse for deep enterprise AI adoption in financial services — once it lands in compliance, the expansion path across the organization is broad.*

- 🔴 **AMD MI450 on TSMC 2nm begins production ramp; OpenAI and Meta as H2 anchor customers** — AMD's next-gen data center GPU is being built on TSMC's 2nm node and has two of the world's largest AI buyers committed to H2 deployments. *If yields hold, Q3 earnings calls will be the first real test of whether AMD can meaningfully challenge Nvidia's data center monopoly.*

- 🏗️ **Nvidia Q1 FY27: $81.6B revenue (+85% YoY), guides Q2 to $89–93B** — Data center alone hit $75.2B; networking surged 263% as NVLink fabric for GB200/GB300 systems ramped. Nvidia is now forecasting revenues approaching $1T across 2026–2027 from Blackwell and Vera Rubin. *The demand signal shows no inflection — every major cloud and enterprise buyer is still in rapid procurement mode.*

- 🔗 **AMD + Nutanix announce full-stack open AI infrastructure platform for agentic workloads** — A multi-year partnership to build an open alternative to Nvidia-centric stacks, specifically targeting agentic AI use cases. *For enterprise infrastructure teams worried about compute lock-in, this is the credible alternative stack to evaluate.*
