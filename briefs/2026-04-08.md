# Morning Download — April 8, 2026

## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

- **U.S.-Iran two-week ceasefire agreed; S&P 500 futures +2.8%, Brent crude -16%.** Pakistan-brokered deal pauses strikes contingent on Iran fully reopening the Strait of Hormuz; markets immediately de-risked. Removes the single biggest volatility driver of the past week and improves the energy-inflation picture — but "two weeks" isn't peace, it's a reload.

- **California asserts independent authority to classify AI startup risk — directly contradicting Trump administration designations.** Newsom says the state will independently evaluate federal supply-chain risk designations; new 2026 CA laws require safety disclosures, whistleblower protections, and chatbot guardrails for minors. Biggest federalism clash in tech regulation right now — California setting de facto national AI standards again.

- **Fed holds rates at 3.5–3.75%; raises 2026 PCE inflation forecast to 2.7%.** One cut still projected for 2026, one for 2027; GDP growth raised to 2.4%. The Iran relief rally is real, but don't expect the Fed to blink — higher inflation expectations keep rates elevated and PE deal economics constrained.

- **McCormick acquires Unilever's food division (Knorr, Hellmann's) in $65B deal; Q1 cross-border M&A up 47% YoY to $454.7B.** Creates a global condiments/seasonings powerhouse across 190+ countries. Traditional corporate consolidation is running alongside the AI deal frenzy.

- **Artemis II crew hits 252,757 miles — farthest humans from Earth since Apollo 13 in 1970.** Splash down April 10 off San Diego. Buried under Iran news, but the first crewed lunar flyby in 50 years is the kind of thing that gets remembered when everything else fades.

- **Wildcard: North Korea fires multiple short-range ballistic missiles for the second consecutive day.** With all eyes on Iran, Pyongyang is quietly stress-testing how much provocative behavior goes uncontested during a multi-front crisis.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

- **MCP crosses 97M monthly installs; Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation formally adopted it; Gartner projects 75% of API gateways support MCP by end of 2026.** OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, AWS all shipping MCP-compatible tooling; Cloudflare now hosts MCP servers natively. The agent interoperability bet is settled — MCP is the USB-C layer. Build MCP-native or build tech debt.

- **Enterprise AI gap: 88% adoption, only 6% fully agentic (Deloitte).** 71% use GenAI but only 33% have scaled it; 39% report measurable EBIT impact. The gap between "we have AI" and "we have working agents" is the primary commercial opportunity for anyone building agentic delivery platforms right now.

- **Model cost collapse: GPT-5.4, Gemini 3.1 Ultra (2M token context), Claude Opus/Sonnet 4.6 (1M context) all live; 12 major updates shipped in one week of March.** What cost $500/month a year ago now costs $50. DeepSeek V3.2 delivers 90% of GPT-5.4 performance at 1/50th the cost. Differentiation windows compressing from quarters to weeks.

- **DeepSeek V4 (1T-parameter MoE) trained for ~$5.2M vs. $100M+ for US frontier models — running on Huawei Ascend 950PR chips, zero NVIDIA.** Expected late April at ~$0.30/MTok. China is validating a fully parallel AI semiconductor stack outside US export controls. If V4 benchmarks hold, it permanently breaks the cost moat of US frontier AI. Story of the month.

- **TSMC advanced capacity (2nm/3nm/5nm) fully booked through 2028 by Nvidia, Apple, AMD, Qualcomm.** Nvidia Vera Rubin (Vera CPU + Rubin GPU on 3nm) enters production H2 2026 with 10x inference cost reduction vs. Blackwell. Hard constraint on AI compute is manufacturing, not architecture — plan for constrained GPU supply through 2028.

- **73% of developers use AI coding tools daily (up from 44% a year ago); Claude Code hits 80.9% SWE-bench.** Google Gemini CLI offers 60 req/min with 1M context free. AI coding tooling is now mainstream developer infrastructure — competition has shifted from "does it work" to "which workflow fits the use case."

- **Wildcard: xAI acquired by SpaceX for $250B — AI consolidation mirrors the cloud bundling playbook.** Grok 4.20 Beta 2 integrates into SpaceX infrastructure. Elon's vertical integration of AI into energy/transport/space is a move hyperscalers haven't made — watch for competitive responses.

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** California's independent AI risk classification framework. Most headlines focus on the ceasefire, but Newsom quietly asserting state-level authority over AI startup risk creates a parallel federal/state compliance maze. Enterprise software companies with California exposure need to track this — CA-specific AI compliance requirements could diverge from federal standards within 30 days.

**One-Liner:** Markets exhaled on the Iran ceasefire while the AI infrastructure war quietly got more expensive, more geopolitical, and more consolidated — all before lunch.
