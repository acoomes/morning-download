New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh inherits an economy that can't tolerate rate cuts but can't afford to stay restrictive forever. His "consensus-building" style will be stress-tested immediately.

Cybersecurity consolidation is accelerating faster than the threat landscape.

🌊 **Wildcard — Ukraine FM: drone strikes on Russia create negotiating pressure** — Energy markets and European defense spending stay elevated, which directly affects data center permitting timelines and power-pricing for cloud operators running EU workloads. Not just a geopolitics story.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🧠 **Anthropic Mythos still a gated preview under Project Glasswing — broader availability "in coming weeks"** — Mythos remains restricted to a small partner cohort while Anthropic works through dual-use risk (cybersecurity researchers have flagged autonomous cyber-ops potential). The underlying capability jump — multi-step strategic reasoning — is real, but the gating, not a general release, is what's setting the regulatory tone for frontier reasoning models.

🔮 **Google Gemini Omni ships with embedded SynthID watermarking** — Google's first Omni-family release unifies multimodal generation under a single model; coverage of input/output modalities at launch is narrower than the long-term any-to-any pitch, with more output modes flagged as future expansion. SynthID provenance watermarking is integrated into supported outputs — Google's bet that content authentication becomes regulatory infrastructure within 18 months.

🤖 **Anthropic Managed Agents: code-execution sandboxes now run on customer-controlled infrastructure** — Day-one sandbox providers: Cloudflare, Daytona, Modal, Vercel. The Claude model and harness orchestration still run on Anthropic's hosted infrastructure — so prompt/session data still egresses for inference — but tool-code execution can now sit inside customer VPCs, which removes a meaningful compliance blocker without eliminating the model-API boundary. **Agentic platform builders: this reshapes the threat-model conversation without fully closing it.**

🔌 **MCP tunnels launch + Anthropic self-hosted sandboxes ship** — MCP tunnels enable cross-network agent communication without VPN hairpins. Combined with VPC sandboxes, MCP is graduating from "developer demo tool" to "enterprise infrastructure primitive." If you're building multi-agent orchestration, this week's changes are load-bearing.

📊 **Microsoft Copilot Studio: 160K orgs, 400K+ agents; Salesforce Agentforce hits $800M ARR** — Enterprise agentic adoption is past the "pilot" phase. These numbers indicate production deployments at scale. The integration race across HR, finance, CRM, and ITSM is the next front — and your competitors' ops teams are probably already running agents.

🛍️ **Meta building "Hatch" personal agent + agentic Instagram shopping tool (internal testing by end of June)** — Meta is playing catch-up as Google and Anthropic pull ahead on personal agents. The Instagram commerce angle is the first major social-commerce agentic play; expect Amazon and TikTok Shop to respond before Q3.

💡 **Anthropic acquires developer-tools startup Stainless** — Stainless builds SDK generation tooling. This is about owning the API ergonomics layer — lower friction to build on Claude means faster developer adoption and stickier enterprise integrations. Read it as infrastructure, not feature.

## Sleeper Story

**TSMC is planning a 15% price hike on 3nm wafers in 2H26, with another 5–10% likely in 2027.** This hasn't hit mainstream enterprise budgets yet, but it will — GPU and custom ASIC costs will flow through to cloud compute pricing within 6–9 months. If your AI infrastructure cost models don't account for this, reprice them now. Monthly 3nm capacity jumped from ~130K to ~175K wafers in Q2, and AI demand is still outrunning it.

The AI stack is graduating from demos to production infrastructure this week — and the compute bill for all of it is about to get significantly more expensive.
