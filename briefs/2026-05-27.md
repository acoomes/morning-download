At $900B, Anthropic overtakes OpenAI's $852B March valuation as the most valuable private AI company on earth.

Spark is a general-purpose agent that reasons across connected apps, in beta for Google AI Ultra subscribers. Google is shipping fast, but the portfolio fragmentation is real.

More significant as an IP and liability story than a raw capability milestone — courts haven't caught up with creative ownership yet.

🔴 **AMD up 5% today, up 114% YTD on 12GW OpenAI + Meta chip contracts** — Q1 data center revenue hit $5.8B (+57% YoY). Wall Street is rotating hard into second-wave AI infrastructure names. 🛠️ **Anthropic acquires Stainless (SDK generation/developer tooling)** — Stainless builds infrastructure for generating and maintaining API SDKs.
