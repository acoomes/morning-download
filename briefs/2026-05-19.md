## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🛡️ **Five Eyes nations jointly issue "Careful Adoption of Agentic AI" guidance** — The US, UK, Australia, Canada, and NZ released coordinated guidance on security risks of agentic AI in critical infrastructure and defense environments; the clearest signal yet that government compliance frameworks for agentic AI are coming. If you're building agentic platforms for enterprise, start building for this now — not later.

🔬 **Anthropic's Project Glasswing surfaces Claude Mythos: zero-days at industrial scale** — A controlled initiative giving select partners (AWS, Apple, Cisco, Google, JPMorgan Chase, Microsoft) access to Claude Mythos Preview already found thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities across every major OS and browser, including a 27-year-old OpenBSD bug. This is the most concrete demonstration yet of AI as a security force multiplier — and a preview of what offensive AI looks like in the wrong hands.

🤖 **Claude Opus 4.7 launches with Claude Design and a Small Business suite** — Better software engineering performance and higher-resolution vision; the new Design product adds visual/prototype outputs; Small Business integrations bring Claude into QuickBooks, HubSpot, Canva, and Microsoft 365. Anthropic is visibly broadening beyond developer-first audiences and going after the SMB layer.

⚡ **Goldman: agentic AI systems will be 60–130x more energy-intensive than today's AI** — Agents use ~4x more compute tokens than standard chat; multi-agent systems use ~15x more; persistent operation multiplies that exponentially. Energy infrastructure is now the hard constraint on agentic AI scale — not models, not data, not talent.

💰 **JPMorgan formally reclassifies AI from R&D experiment to core infrastructure** — $19.8B tech budget and 2,000 dedicated AI staff signals the inflection from "pilot" to "operating model" at the largest US bank; expect this framing to cascade across financial services over the next two quarters.

🔧 **MCP 2026 roadmap: stateless HTTP transport and webhooks unlock production scale** — Horizontal scaling without persistent SSE connections is the fix production engineering teams have been waiting for; this unblocks enterprise-grade agentic deployments that couldn't tolerate stateful connection management. Governance via the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, AWS co-founders) is maturing fast.

💻 **AMD MI450 (TSMC 2nm) ramping H2 2026 with OpenAI and Meta as anchor customers** — NVIDIA has locked up most of TSMC's advanced packaging capacity, but AMD is building real enterprise footing; TSMC stock is up 33% YTD, outpacing even NVIDIA's gains.
