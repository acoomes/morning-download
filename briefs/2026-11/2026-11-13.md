The index damage was small because the selling was concentrated in one name. Do not mistake a calm close for a calm market.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🚨 **Nvidia's board announced Jensen Huang's resignation on Thursday, November 12; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and Harvey Jones, a board member since 1993 and former Synopsys CEO, is the new chair** — The board met Wednesday evening after reports about Huang's affairs, one with a woman U.S. intelligence suspects of spying for China, and will weigh internal and external candidates for a permanent successor. This ends the "no announced outcome" state of Thursday's briefing. **For enterprise AI delivery:** the founder who set the roadmap, allocation priorities and customer relationships is gone mid-cycle, so ask your GPU supplier or cloud provider in writing whether delivery commitments change under an interim chief.

Sources: [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns/) · [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/12/nx-s1-6037833/jensen-huang-nvidia-board-fired) · [SF Chronicle](https://www.sfchronicle.com/tech/article/nvidia-jensen-huang-fired-affair-22525954.php) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-140651041.html)

📉 **Nvidia plunged about 14% at the open and finished down 4.3%, while AMD rose 5.9% and Broadcom gained 5.5%** — Investors halved the damage within hours, which says they see the franchise, not the founder, as the asset. The AMD and Broadcom gains show where money goes when Nvidia wobbles: the alternatives. **For platform builders:** a multi-vendor accelerator strategy just got easier to justify to a board, and AMD and Broadcom silicon is the obvious second source to price.

Sources: [New Straits Times / Reuters](https://www.nst.com.my/business/corporate/2026/11/1576568/wall-street-ends-lower-nvidia-ceo-resigns-us-yields)

🛡️ **Bay emerged from stealth on November 10 with $50 million in combined seed and Series A funding, led by Battery Ventures, for a security and control platform for autonomous enterprise agents** — Battery led the $37 million Series A portion. **For anyone building agentic platforms:** agent governance and policing is turning into its own funded layer, next to sandboxes and orchestration. I found Bay's round only in a secondary write-up, so check the company's own announcement before quoting it.

Sources: [Pomegra](https://pomegra.io/startups/bay-raises-50m-to-police-enterprise-ai-agents-in-2026-2026-11-13)

🧠 **Graphon Labs raised a $35 million Series A, led by RRE Ventures, for a memory layer that gives AI agents long-term recall** — Total funding is now $43.3 million. Persistent memory is one of the unsolved pieces in production agent design, since most agents still start each task with a blank slate. The round is small, but it sits where builders are actually stuck.

Sources: [WOWTALE](https://en.wowtale.net/2026/11/12/235967/)

📊 **Nebuly, based in Turin, raised a $21 million Series A led by Planven to measure the business return on enterprise AI** — **For enterprise AI delivery:** with long yields above 5%, buyers want proof of payback, and a vendor category has formed around supplying it. Expect ROI instrumentation to show up in your next statement of work whether you propose it or not.

Sources: [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/11/12/nebuly-raises-21m-series-a-to-turn-ai-investments-into-measurable-results)

🎲 **Wildcard: Huang's resignation hit China's AI stack harder than America's** — Alibaba's 7.1% drop against AMD's and Broadcom's gains shows the market sorting winners by access to non-Nvidia compute and to U.S. hardware, not by model quality. Watch whether Beijing pushes domestic accelerators as the Nvidia hardware outlook gets murkier.

Sources: [New Straits Times / Reuters](https://www.nst.com.my/business/corporate/2026/11/1576568/wall-street-ends-lower-nvidia-ceo-resigns-us-yields)

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** The agent-governance layer is getting funded faster than anyone is buying it. Why it matters: Bay's security and control platform, Graphon's memory layer and Nebuly's ROI measurement all raised money within the same few days, each aimed at a problem enterprise buyers raise after the pilot works. If you are shipping agents to a customer within 30 days, expect their security and finance teams to ask who polices the agent, what it remembers and what it returned, so have answers ready.

**One-Liner:** Nvidia lost its founder and the index barely blinked, but the 2-year yield did not forget what the Fed might do next.
