## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** OpenAI's Agent Builder and Evals shut down on November 30. Why it matters: OpenAI announced the wind-down on June 3 and recommends the Agents SDK for workflows that should continue as code; third-party guides say you must export a workflow from Agent Builder before the shutdown because the export option disappears with it. Sources disagree on whether hosted ChatKit workflows that depend on Agent Builder will keep running, so treat that as unresolved and check OpenAI's notice. Within the next 30 days, any customer who built agents in the visual builder has a hard migration deadline. Delivery teams should inventory those workflows now. It also fits today's LangChain money: the durable layer is the one you own and can export.

**One-Liner:** Nvidia's CFO is running the company, Anthropic is about to list, and the market is asking whether the AI bill gets paid.
