## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🔐 **OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber on November 5, limited to approved Daybreak Red customers through its API and Codex** — OpenAI's own ExploitGym evaluation scores it at 48.5% versus 32.1% for GPT-5.6 Cyber, and OpenAI rates its vulnerability-finding and exploit-development ability "critical," up from "high." The company says researchers used it to find 948 Linux privilege-escalation exploits and 81 previously unknown high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities. Reported out-of-scope rates differ by outlet (under 1% in one, 0.07% of runs without deployment safety instructions in another), and OpenAI declined to describe what happened in those runs. **For platform builders:** a vendor is shipping a restricted, scope-adhering offensive-security agent, which previews how gated access and scope enforcement will be sold to enterprises. All figures are OpenAI-reported.

Sources: [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/openai-releases-gpt-6-cyber-with-better-scope-adherence-f89a9831) · [eSecurity Planet](https://www.esecurityplanet.com/news/news-openai-gpt-6-cyber-security-benchmarks/) · [StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/news/openai-gpt-6-cyber-firefox-linux-flaws/)

📈 **METR published an assessment on November 5 estimating that Anthropic is improving its models' capabilities on well-specified tasks at roughly double its March 2024 to October 2025 rate, with high uncertainty** — METR ran a high-access evaluation at Anthropic from August 12 to October 29 and attributes most of the acceleration to Anthropic's own use of AI in R&D. The caveat is large: Anthropic redacted most of the evidence METR found most informative, including the detailed estimates of AI's effect on its rate of progress. Outlets quote the multiple as about 1.9x and as a range of 1.3x to 3x. **For enterprise AI delivery:** if frontier labs are accelerating, plan model-upgrade cycles and evaluation harnesses for faster churn, and do not hard-wire to a single model version.

Sources: [METR](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment) · [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91636791/anthropic-metr-ai-claude-acceleration)

🎲 **Wildcard: Anthropic launched Personal Finance in Claude on November 5, a US beta for Pro, Max and Ultra subscribers that reads linked bank and card accounts but cannot move money** — Accounts connect through Plaid, so users sign in with their bank rather than giving Claude a password. Claude gets read-only access to balances, credit limits and up to two years of transaction history. Anthropic says conversations in the hub are excluded from model training, there are no ads or sponsored recommendations, and it calls this "a first step" toward working with licensed professionals. **For builders:** this is a clean example of the pattern enterprises will ask for, a consent-based connector with read-only scope and explicit data-use terms, applied to the most sensitive consumer data there is.

Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-claude-personal-finance-plaid-read-only-beta) · [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/05/anthropic-upgrades-claude-with-new-personal-finance-feature/)
