## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** The December hearing calendar colliding with a Fed that is leaning toward a hike. Why it matters: Senate Banking has asked Jensen Huang to testify publicly on December 3 and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick on December 10, with subpoenas threatened. Both land inside the window when markets are already skittish and hike odds were above 80% on the last reading I could source. If you run AI workloads on cloud capacity, a bad hearing or a hot data print could land on the same fragile tape, so decide now what spend you would pause first.

**One-Liner:** Wall Street fell off a cliff, climbed back before the bell, and then went home for turkey with nobody quite sure what it had just seen.
