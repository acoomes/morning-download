## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** The BIS remote-access rule's reach into corporate structure and cloud rentals. Why it matters: reporting says the rule targets workarounds through subsidiaries, restructuring and remote cloud access, and that it sets license requirements tied to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil. I could not open the rule text, so confirm which requirements turn on a shipment's destination and which on the recipient's headquarters. One commentary questions whether BIS has clear statutory authority for remote-access controls without the Remote Access Security Act, which has passed the House but has not become law. Within 30 days, expect the Federal Register publication date, a comment period and the first provider notices to customers. Anyone buying GPU capacity from a regional cloud should ask for the licensing position in writing before the next contract renewal.

**One-Liner:** Nvidia posted a blowout quarter, Anthropic is about to list, and Washington chose the same week to tighten the leash on where the chips can run.
