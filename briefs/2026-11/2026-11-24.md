## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

📈 **Wall Street ripped higher on Monday, November 23: the Nasdaq Composite rose 6.51% to 24,089.56, the S&P 500 gained 4.73% to about 7,237, and the Dow added 1,447.98 points, or 3.01%, to 49,595.31** — Nvidia, up roughly 31% on the day, dragged semiconductor, memory and neocloud stocks up with it, and the Nasdaq and S&P posted their best sessions since April 2025. One personnel decision moved the whole tape. If your portfolio or your customers' budgets are tied to AI sentiment, treat this as a single-stock governance event, not a change in fundamentals. Futures were already fading on Tuesday morning, with S&P 500 contracts down about 0.5% and Nasdaq 100 contracts down nearly 1.5%.

Sources: [STL.News](https://www.stl.news/us-stock-market-today-november-23-2026/) · [European Financial Review](https://www.europeanfinancialreview.com/stocks-surge-as-nvidia-brings-back-jensen-huang-nasdaq-up-6-5-dow-gains-1448-points/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)

🏛️ **Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director on Monday, less than a week after Chair Harvey Jones said it did not intend to bring him back** — An SEC filing says the vote was unanimous, that the board received input from senior levels of the U.S. government, and that Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort; the independent committee's investigation continues to conclusion. Huang had resigned on November 12 amid reports about his relationship with a woman U.S. intelligence suspects of spying for China. The market is cheering the return; the open question is what the investigation finds. Expect Congress to keep pressing on export-control compliance.

Sources: [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000090/nov23pressrelease.htm) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/nvidia-jensen-huang-politics-market.html) · [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/17/tech/nvidia-earnings-jensen-huang-exit)

💼 **CrowdStrike agreed to buy Zscaler in an all-stock deal valuing Zscaler at about $43.9 billion, with Zscaler holders receiving 1.135 CrowdStrike shares per share** — Based on CrowdStrike's November 20 close the deal implies about $260.41 per Zscaler share, a premium of roughly 34%, and CrowdStrike holders would own about 84.1% of the combined company. Zscaler founder Jay Chaudhry and affiliates, holding about 34% of the shares, signed voting agreements, and Zscaler could owe a $1.4 billion termination fee in certain circumstances. CrowdStrike expects closing in the second half of its fiscal 2028. **For enterprise-software readers:** endpoint and network-edge security are consolidating into one platform vendor, which matters for anyone selling security controls around agent deployments.

Sources: [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001535527/000153552726000035/crwd-20261123xex991.htm)

🧬 **Sanofi agreed to acquire Insmed for $175 per share in cash, about $41.0 billion** — The deal brings Brinsupri and Arikayce plus a late-stage respiratory program, TPIP, and it lands as Sanofi faces a Dupixent patent cliff. This is the day's biggest deal by dollars and almost irrelevant to enterprise software. Flagging it so you can ignore it.

Sources: [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001121404/000119312526473314/d130092dex991.htm)

🎲 **Wildcard: Trump reportedly called into an Nvidia board meeting to argue for Huang's return, and Rep. Reed has asked for a transcript** — According to Semafor reporting relayed by CNBC, the president personally lobbied a private company's board, which then reversed its public position within days. Whatever you think of the outcome, the precedent is that a chip supplier's CEO is now a matter of national-policy bargaining. Plan vendor-concentration risk accordingly.

Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/nvidia-jensen-huang-politics-market.html)

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🔥 **The Huang reversal ripples through compute: the rebound lifted chips, memory and neocloud stocks, and analysts at Melius, Bernstein and StoneX upgraded Nvidia** — Monday's move shows how much of the AI trade is priced on one executive's continuity, not on orders. **For enterprise AI delivery:** if your roadmap assumes Nvidia capacity and pricing stay stable, last week's volatility is a reminder to keep a second-source option in the plan.

Sources: [European Financial Review](https://www.europeanfinancialreview.com/stocks-surge-as-nvidia-brings-back-jensen-huang-nasdaq-up-6-5-dow-gains-1448-points/) · [STL.News](https://www.stl.news/us-stock-market-today-november-23-2026/)

🧠 **Google launched Gemini 4 Neon on November 18, pitched as the mid-tier model for tool-heavy, long-running agent work, and Antigravity's Orchestrator now pairs Neon as planner with Gemini 4 Hydrogen as executor in a single session** — Google says Neon can act as an orchestrator directing parallel Hydrogen subagents and ranks first on Zapier's AutomationBench at 56.3%, a vendor-reported figure I could not independently verify. It is available in the Gemini API in AI Studio, in Gemini for business and, for paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, in Antigravity. **For platform builders:** planner-plus-cheap-executor is becoming a first-party product pattern, not just something you wire up yourself in an orchestration framework. This launch is six days old rather than inside the past 24 hours; I included it because the window was otherwise empty.

Sources: [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-neon/) · [Google Antigravity](https://antigravity.google/blog/gemini-4-neon-in-google-antigravity/)

🏭 **Nvidia said on November 18 it is expanding commitments with TSMC and other supply-chain partners across substrates, advanced packaging and wafers** — As reported, production is expected to begin in the second half of 2027, with the additional capacity targeted for the third quarter of 2028. That is a long way out. Packaging and substrates, not leading-edge wafers, remain the constraint to watch, so near-term GPU supply for your deployments will not change.

Sources: [TechPowerUp](https://www.techpowerup.com/354985/nvidia-expands-commitments-with-supply-chain-partners-to-support-ai-infrastructure-buildout)

🎲 **Wildcard: agents are getting wallets** — Weekly roundups report that OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, which lets agents pay without exposing card details, and that Google turned Gemini's task feature into an agent that can place calls and make purchases under a spending cap. I could not confirm either from a primary source, so treat both as reported. **For agentic builders:** payment authorization, spend caps and audit trails are becoming product requirements. If you build agent platforms, design the approval and ledger layer now.

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** Nvidia's independent investigation, which the board says will run to conclusion despite Huang's reinstatement. Why it matters: the market priced a clean return on Monday, but the filing itself says Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort, and a congressional request for the board-call transcript is already open. Over the next 30 days, a finding, a leak or an export-control action could reverse a 31% one-day move, and every enterprise AI plan that assumes stable Nvidia supply inherits that risk. The CrowdStrike–Zscaler tie-up is the quieter counterpart: security vendors are consolidating just as agents start spending money.

**One-Liner:** The most valuable company in AI un-resigned its CEO over a weekend, the Nasdaq rewarded it with a 6.5% day, and everyone politely agreed not to read the footnotes.
