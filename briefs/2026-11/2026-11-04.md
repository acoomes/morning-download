BNY's pre-election note says Democrats have signaled expanded AI oversight, including a possible House select committee, while Trump's opposition limits major federal rules and keeps state-level action important. **For enterprise AI delivery:** expect more procurement questions about audit trails, model provenance and data use, driven by oversight hearings rather than statute.

If you ship an agent, ACP support is the cheapest distribution you can buy, and if you build a platform, design for being replaceable.

Sources: [Android Developers Blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/09/build-your-way-use-any-ai-agent-in-android-studio.html) · [Developers Digest](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/android-studio-rabbit-2-claude-codex-acp-android-cli-plugin-2026)
