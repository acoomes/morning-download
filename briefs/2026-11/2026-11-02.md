## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

🗳️ **Midterms are Tuesday, November 3, and investors are treating Congress as an AI risk factor** — Market watchers see a Democratic win in both chambers as the biggest AI-related political threat, because it would bring more scrutiny of AI and data centers. Federal AI legislation is considered unlikely before the vote, so the real action is local: New York imposed a one-year moratorium on new hyperscale data centers in July, and state and local officials control the permits. **For enterprise-software readers:** the policy risk to your AI roadmap is less a federal statute than a county board refusing a power hookup. Analysts also note that midterm years tend to bring higher volatility, so do not read Wednesday's tape as a verdict.

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** The local-level data center fight. Why it matters: with federal AI legislation stalled, state and county decisions on moratoria and permits — such as New York's one-year hyperscale pause — now set the pace of AI capacity, and Tuesday's state and local results could shift that within weeks. If you sell AI infrastructure or run capacity planning, watch which jurisdictions elect data-center skeptics before the next capex guidance cycle.

**One-Liner:** Wall Street heads into Apple earnings, the midterms and a jobs report with an AI trade it no longer trusts and a regulatory map it cannot yet read.
