## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

🌏 **Trump, Xi and Putin held their first formal three-way meeting on Thursday, November 19, in Shenzhen on the sidelines of APEC, and left with modest results** — No agreement to reduce nuclear arsenals was confirmed, and the joint statements did not address the future of Taiwan or Ukraine. China and Russia said the three sides agreed on UN reforms, boosting fuel supplies and a dialogue on nuclear issues. Xi called the talks "positive, constructive and fruitful" and Putin called them "useful and substantive." Reports differ on the session's length (about 80 to 90 minutes). For enterprise-software and chip readers, the useful signal is the absence of any trade or technology-control deal: export-control and tariff exposure stays exactly where it was going into the weekend.

🎲 **Wildcard: one position the three leaders did share was that the International Criminal Court has overstepped its jurisdiction** — After the trilateral, the three took aim at the ICC, alongside the UN reform, fuel supply and nuclear dialogue points above. A summit whose joint statements are silent on Taiwan and Ukraine but which still produces a shared line against an international court shows where the common interest sits. Critics quoted in coverage worry the format points toward spheres of influence.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🤖 **Google made Gemini Tasks, its task-completion agent, free in six countries on November 17: the U.S., Canada, Japan, South Korea, Australia and India** — Core Tasks mode is free in all six, but the advanced pieces are US-first: phone calling is US-only, and Google Pay agentic checkout, built on the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2), initially targets eligible US users. Rollout is staged, so some accounts will see it later. **For platform builders:** a consumer-scale agent is now shipping with a payments protocol attached, which makes AP2-style agent checkout a design constraint for any commerce or procurement agent you build, not a future option. Some secondary outlets attach a model name to the release that I could not confirm against Google's own post, so I have left it out.

Sources: [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-google-gemini-tasks-free-six-countries/) · [Digital Trends](https://digitaltrends.com/cool-tech/google-gemini-tasks-free)
