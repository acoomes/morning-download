## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🔌 **Nvidia closed the week down nearly 13% after Jensen Huang's exit, with the CFO as interim chief, and reports Tuesday** — The move was driven by leadership turmoil, not by any change in demand data. **For enterprise AI delivery:** nothing in Friday's tape shows customers cutting orders, but Tuesday's results are the first hard read. If GPU supply is on your critical path, get delivery commitments in writing before the print, not after it.

Sources: [Finwire](https://finwire.io/news/stock-markets-news/us-stock-futures-rise-after-nvidia-ceo-exit-spooks-wall-st) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/p-500-dow-nasdaq-end-233729977.html)

🔮 **Thinking Machines Lab launched Inkling 2 Small on November 5, a 594-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 17 billion active parameters, available on its Tinker platform with weights closed at launch** — The lab said it planned to publish the weights under Apache 2.0 about a week later, after external safety testing and red-teaming; I could not confirm that release has happened, so check before you rely on it. **For platform builders:** a customizable model with an open-weights path gives you a second-source option for fine-tuned agent workloads, but until the weights ship it is a hosted dependency. This is last week's launch, not a new one; it is here because the open-weights step is the part still pending.

Sources: [WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/thinking-machines-inkling-2-small-tinker-apache-weights/) · [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/thinking-machines-lab-releases-inkling-2-small-a-594b-moe-mo-2b2a96ce)

🤖 **Insight Enterprises launched an online Agent Store on November 13, giving clients a catalog of proven AI solutions** — A large IT reseller packaging agents as a catalog item is a sign that enterprise buyers want agents purchased like software, with a short list and vendor vetting. **For enterprise AI delivery:** resellers are moving into the layer between your product and the CIO's procurement team, so expect your agents to be compared against catalog entries.

Sources: [AIwire](https://hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/11/13/insight-launches-agent-store-to-accelerate-enterprise-ai-adoption)

🧪 **Plurai launched general availability of a free agent simulator on November 11 alongside a $13 million seed round led by Team8** — The tool simulates conversations to test agents before they go live. **For anyone building agentic platforms:** pre-production simulation is turning into its own product category, next to sandboxes, governance and memory. Plurai's accuracy claims come from its own announcement and have not been independently audited.

Sources: [i10x](https://i10x.ai/news/plurai-simulation-agent-testing-13-million-seed)

🎲 **Wildcard: TSMC is reportedly evaluating advanced memory production, including stacked 3D DRAM, at Nvidia's request** — The report comes from one outlet, 247WallSt, citing two sources, and neither company has confirmed it. If true, the foundry that makes the logic would also start making the memory that limits how fast it can be used, which is a bigger shift in the supply chain than any single chip announcement. Treat it as a rumor until TSMC or Nvidia comments.

Sources: [247WallSt](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/09/taiwan-semiconductor-wants-to-enter-into-advanced-memory-production/)

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** Agent testing is becoming a product. Why it matters: Plurai's free simulator and $13 million seed arrive in the same week as governance, memory and ROI-measurement startups, which suggests buyers now ask how an agent was tested before they ask what it can do. If you ship an agent to a customer within 30 days, expect a request for evidence of pre-production testing, so have a simulation or evaluation report ready.

**One-Liner:** The market shrugged off Nvidia's worst week since April, and now it waits for Tuesday to find out whether it was right to.
