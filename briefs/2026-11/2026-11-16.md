🎲 **Wildcard: United Airlines revised its contract of carriage on November 11 to bar unauthorized third-party AI agents from booking, changing or servicing tickets, even when the customer approves what the agent does** — This is not from the last 24 hours, but it is the cleanest example yet of a large consumer brand choosing to gate agent access. Sources: [Anthropic](https://anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch) · [RTTNews](https://www.rttnews.com/3712632/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-at-70-78-per-share-launches-roadshow.aspx?type=bn)

🪪 **The Linux Foundation launched the Agent Name Service on November 10 as a hosted project, an open standard that uses DNS and domains organizations already own to give AI agents verifiable identity and discovery** — A technical steering committee will govern the specification. GoDaddy built and ran the original before handing it over. Critics, including The New Stack, argue that it inherits a fragile DNS trust hierarchy and is pushed hardest by the registrars who would profit from it. **For platform builders:** pair this with United's ban on unidentified agents. If merchants want to know which agent is knocking, an identity layer is where that answer will come from, and now there is a neutral standard to build against or argue with.

Sources: [Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-welcomes-agent-name-service-to-bring-verifiable-identity-and-discovery-to-ai-agents) · [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/can-dns-become-the-basis-for-ai-agent-identity) · [Diginomica](https://diginomica.com/agent-identity-gets-dns-anchor-how-godaddy-built-agent-name-service-then-gave-it-linux-foundation)

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** The agent-identity gate. Why it matters: United's rule lets agents show fares but not transact, and the Linux Foundation's Agent Name Service offers a neutral way for an agent to prove who it is. If more merchants copy United, agent platforms that cannot identify themselves will find checkout and servicing flows closed to them, while platforms that can will get privileged access. Within the next 30 days, expect other travel and retail brands to publish agent policies, and expect your own customers to ask how your agents authenticate.

**One-Liner:** The market is waiting on Nvidia and Anthropic to say the AI bill is worth paying, while United is saying a stranger's agent cannot spend it.
