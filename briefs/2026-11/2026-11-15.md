## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

✈️ **The FAA issued a nationwide ground stop on Friday, November 13, citing communication issues at multiple Air Route Traffic Control Centers, and lifted it the same evening** — The agency said technicians were investigating. Reports quote Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy blaming overloaded routers on an "incredibly old" system, and Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin saying there was no evidence of a cyberattack and that officials were examining incompatibilities between old equipment and newer technology being installed. Neither official named a specific trigger. Treat the cause as open: a single point of failure that can halt every U.S. departure is an infrastructure-resilience story, not a one-day travel story.

Sources: [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/faa-statement-nationwide-ground-stop) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/13/faa-nationwide-ground-stop) · [NBC Chicago](https://www.nbcchicago.com/news/local/faa-issues-nationwide-ground-stop-due-to-extraordinary-circumstances-and-safety/4016516/) · [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa)

🪟 **A global Microsoft 365 outage that began Thursday, November 12 at about 16:00 GMT knocked out Outlook and disrupted Teams and Copilot in 74 countries, and was still recovering on Friday** — Microsoft said the cause was not malicious. One outlet attributes it to a network capacity upgrade; I found that in a single source, so treat it as unconfirmed until Microsoft publishes its post-incident report. **For enterprise-software readers:** if your agents or your customers' workflows sit on Copilot or Microsoft 365, this is your live-fire test of fallback behavior. Ask what your agents do when the mail and chat layer disappears for a day.

Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/13/microsoft-hit-by-global-outages-what-happened-and-why) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/massive-microsoft-365-outage-disrupts-outlook-teams-copilot-worldwide/) · [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/live-blog/microsoft-outage-outlook-teams-updates-fix-rcna609118) · [JournalArta](https://journalarta.com/en/2026/11/14/microsoft-services-still-returning-365-outage/)

🏛️ **New York opened its registration portal on November 10 for large frontier AI developers, who must register by January 1, 2027** — The threshold is more than $500 million in annual gross revenue, counting affiliates together, and OpenAI and Anthropic are the companies most outlets name. This is not from the last 24 hours, but it is the first hard compliance date for a state frontier-AI regime. **For enterprise AI delivery:** expect your model vendors to start passing registration and safety-reporting obligations down into contracts.

Sources: [NY DFS](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/digit/Frontier-Developers) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/new-york-opens-ai-portal-openai-anthropic-must-file-by-jan-1)

🛢️ **Iran's navy said an oil tanker caught fire on Saturday morning after hitting a naval mine in the eastern Strait of Hormuz** — The claim comes from Iranian state media as relayed in the Times of Israel liveblog; I could not corroborate it independently. If confirmed, mines in a shipping lane keep energy risk premiums in play for the inflation numbers that already have markets worried about the Fed.

Sources: [Times of Israel liveblog](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-november-14-2026/)

🎲 **Wildcard: SpaceX caught a Starship upper stage with the launch tower on Saturday, November 14, a first** — Ship 42 was caught by the tower's arms at Starbase about 22.5 hours after launch. The Super Heavy booster was lost: it was diverted to the Gulf of Mexico and exploded before hitting the water. One of two attempts landing is a good day for a program built on iterating through failures, and ship reuse is the prerequisite for the cadence Starship's economics assume. Exact timing differs slightly between outlets.

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** The FAA ground stop's unresolved root cause. Why it matters: officials pointed to overloaded routers and to incompatibilities between old equipment and newer technology being installed, without naming a trigger. If the cause is an upgrade-path flaw, the same condition exists everywhere that equipment is being swapped. Expect an FAA findings update, and expect enterprise teams to take the lesson that a modernization rollout is a resilience risk in its own right, a lesson the Microsoft outage reinforced the same week.

**One-Liner:** The week's infrastructure kept failing in single big pieces, while Starship caught one on the way down.
