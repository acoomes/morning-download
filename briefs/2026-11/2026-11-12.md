## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

📈 **Stocks bounced hard on Wednesday, November 11: the S&P 500 rose 1.31% to 7,619.38, the Nasdaq gained 1.60% to 25,466.19, and the Dow added about 0.5% to near 52,486** — CoreWeave's earnings revived the AI trade and chip stocks rallied, erasing Tuesday's hot-CPI selloff. Stocks traded on Veterans Day while the U.S. bond market was closed, so the rally happened without a price check from Treasuries. Treat it as a relief bounce until the bond market reopens and weighs in.

Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) · [CAPP Notes](https://cappnotes.substack.com/p/closing-look-wednesday-november-11)

🏦 **The 10-year Treasury yield briefly touched 5% in European trading while U.S. bond markets were shut, and the dollar hit a 19-month high** — Tuesday's hotter-than-expected October CPI pushed odds of a Fed rate hike higher, and Wednesday's equity rally did not change that math. **For enterprise-software readers:** with money this expensive, expect CFOs to keep demanding payback timelines on AI spend. The real test of Wednesday's rally is Thursday's bond session.

Sources: [Media Selangor](https://mediaselangor.com/en/2026/11/406483) · [Schwab](https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/stock-market-update-open)

🛡️ **President Trump reportedly approved new export restrictions aimed at companies suspected of diverting Nvidia AI chips to China, after a daylong standoff with Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick** — Reports say the measures have not been announced and that Megaspeed's possible addition to the Entity List is undecided. Separately, The Information reportedly says Commerce is drafting rules that would tighten AI chip licensing for Singapore, Malaysia and Thailand to stop Chinese firms renting compute through local data centers. Both items rest on secondary and aggregator reporting, so treat the details as unconfirmed until Commerce publishes something. **If you deliver AI in Southeast Asia:** a licensing change there would hit your capacity plans first.

Sources: [Tech Trends News Update](https://techtrendsnewsupdate.substack.com/p/trump-authorizes-crackdown-on-nvidia) · [TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/newsletter/144292) · [JKN](https://news.jkn.co.kr/editions/english/post/1088257)

🏛️ **Three Republican House committee chairs asked Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang to appear for a joint closed-door briefing on November 18** — The request follows the board's independent investigation into Huang's relationship with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive. Reporting cites people briefed on U.S. intelligence, via The New York Times, saying she was recruited by China's Ministry of State Security; those claims are unproven. Congress is now an actor in a story that began as a corporate governance matter.

Sources: [Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/news/nation-world/nvidia-jensen-huang-future-megaspeed-20261112.html) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/330287/20261109/jensen-huang-admits-affair-nvidia-board-probes-reports-chinese-spy-targeted-ceo.htm)

🌀 **Wildcard: Storm Charles reached mainland Portugal, with a headline reporting more than 200 flight cancellations and school closures** — Euronews had forecast torrential rain and gusts up to 120 km/h, and this is the third named storm of the 2026/27 season. I could only see the headline of the disruption report, so I am not citing specific figures beyond it. It is a reminder that Europe's winter storm season is already running at an aggressive cadence.

Sources: [104.9 The Wolf](https://www.1049thewolf.com/2026/11/11/portugal-cancels-200-plus-flights-shuts-schools-as-storm-charles-hits/) · [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/09/storm-charles-to-hit-portugal-and-spain-with-torrential-rain-and-120-kmh-winds)

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🚨 **After Wednesday's close, CNBC cited a Wall Street Journal report about Nvidia's board and Jensen Huang, and the stock fell roughly 8.6% in after-hours trading** — The board met Wednesday evening to discuss Huang's future, according to NBC News, and the White House was reportedly aware of the meeting; as of Thursday morning there was no announced outcome, per AP. Nvidia had closed up about 2.9% earlier in the day after two down sessions. The next hard dates are the November 17 earnings call and the November 18 House briefing. **For anyone building on Nvidia's roadmap:** leadership risk at the dominant compute supplier is now a planning input, not a headline.

Sources: [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/stock-market-today-live-november-11-2026) · [Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/news/nation-world/nvidia-jensen-huang-future-megaspeed-20261112.html) · [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/330287/20261109/jensen-huang-admits-affair-nvidia-board-probes-reports-chinese-spy-targeted-ceo.htm)

☁️ **CoreWeave reported third-quarter revenue of $3.66 billion, up 168% from $1.37 billion, and raised its 2026 revenue outlook to $13 billion–$13.3 billion** — Its backlog stands at about $132.5 billion, and the stock surged as much as 25% on Wednesday. The cost side is the other half of the story: net loss widened to $709 million, net interest expense climbed to $846 million, and reports cite about $47 billion of debt. CEO Michael Intrator also told CNBC he expects no impact on completing the U.S. facilities that back the existing backlog. **For enterprise AI delivery:** demand for GPU capacity is still outrunning supply, but the neocloud model runs on leverage, so check a provider's financing before you sign multi-year capacity.

Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/coreweave-stock-surges-as-q3-revenue-beats-estimates-company-raises-full-year-outlook-193338179.html) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/coreweave-raises-2026-outlook-13-081920985.html) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/crwv-stock-climbs-8-hours-220940004.html)

🧠 **Memory stocks Micron and Sandisk soared after CoreWeave's CEO said AI demand outpaces the world's capacity** — I could see only the headline of this report, so I cannot detail the move. The read-through is that the supply chain around GPUs, not just the GPUs themselves, is where the scarcity is showing up. If your agent platform depends on large-context inference, expect memory costs to feature in capacity pricing.

Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/micron-sandisk-soar-coreweave-ceo-212511602.html)

🤖 **Rox, a San Francisco startup that builds AI agents for sales, raised $100 million in a Series B at a $1.3 billion valuation on November 11** — General Catalyst and March Capital co-led, with Sequoia and Factory participating, per Reuters. The company says annual recurring revenue grew more than tenfold in its first year on the market (a company claim) and plans to spend on AI research, custom models and enterprise verticals such as healthcare and financial services. **For platform builders:** vertical agents with a named workflow, here sales, are the category still drawing large rounds, and Rox's custom-model plan is a bet that general models alone will not hold the moat.

Sources: [TradingView / Reuters](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2026:newsml_L3N46S0ZJ:0-automation-startup-rox-raises-100-million-at-1-3-billion-valuation/)

🔌 **Google's Open Source Blog has a November 2026 post on sandboxing untrusted AI agents at scale, built on its Agent Substrate runtime for Kubernetes** — Agent Substrate is already available on GKE; the post argues that stateful agents break conventional container assumptions and ties the runtime to enterprise governance. A third-party write-up describes a demo of roughly 250 stateful agent sessions on 8 pods, and InfoQ covered Google's AX orchestrator, which runs on top of it, in September. I could not open the post and cannot confirm its exact date, so this is context, not a fresh launch. **For platform builders:** execution isolation and state snapshotting are being standardized on Kubernetes, and that sets the baseline your own agent runtime gets compared against.

Sources: [Google Open Source Blog](https://opensource.googleblog.com/2026/11/sandboxing-untrusted-ai-agents-at-scale.html) · [Solo.io](https://www.solo.io/topics/ai-infrastructure/how-google-agent-substrate-works) · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/09/google-ax-orchestrator/) · [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/containers-kubernetes/agent-substrate-available-on-gke)

🎲 **Wildcard: Singapore-based Megaspeed pledged to cooperate with a U.S. investigation into potential national security risks** — The company said it takes U.S. export control compliance "extremely seriously." No public finding has established that Megaspeed illegally diverted chips to China, and the intelligence claims about Alice Huang remain unproven. A mid-size Singapore reseller is now the pivot between Nvidia's board, Congress and Commerce's export rules.

Sources: [Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/news/nation-world/nvidia-jensen-huang-future-megaspeed-20261112.html)

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** The collision of three calendars around Nvidia and chip export policy. Why it matters: the November 17 earnings call, the November 18 closed-door House briefing and any Commerce action on chip diversion or Southeast Asian data-center licensing could all land within a week of each other. If you buy GPU capacity through resellers or Southeast Asian data centers, ask your provider now where the chips are licensed and what happens to your contract if a rule changes.

**One-Liner:** Stocks forgot about inflation for a day because the AI backlog got bigger, and then Nvidia's boardroom reminded everyone that the backlog has a single point of failure.
