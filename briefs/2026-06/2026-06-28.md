## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🧠 **OpenAI’s model churn is now the story: GPT-5.5 Instant is the current ChatGPT default** — GPT-5.5 Instant became the new ChatGPT default in May 2026; GPT-5.2 is already retired. The architectural innovation in this family — a real-time complexity router that auto-escalates to deeper reasoning based on query intent — changes how you’d design multi-step workflows. But if you’re standardizing on any specific OpenAI model version, pin it explicitly: the default changes under you faster than most enterprise review cycles.

🔮 **Agentic Resource Discovery (ARD) spec launches — the “DNS for agents”** — Co-authored by Google, Microsoft, and Hugging Face (June 17), backed by Amazon, Cisco, Salesforce, Snowflake, GitHub, and NVIDIA. ARD standardizes dynamic discovery of tools, MCP servers, APIs, and agents at runtime via federated ai-catalog.json registries. For multi-agent platform builders: this is the missing infrastructure primitive — it eliminates the “pre-loading 1,000 static tools” context pollution problem plaguing production deployments today.

🤖 **AI agent spend hits $206B in 2026 (Gartner), but readiness and rollback data tell a harder story** — Fivetran’s 2026 Agentic AI Readiness Index found only 15% of organizations are fully production-ready; separately, Sinch research on customer-service agent deployments found high rollback rates specifically in that use case. The pattern holds: governance and auditability are the differentiators, not model capability. Lead with durability and rollback mechanisms — not benchmarks.

🔌 **NVIDIA + TSMC deploy AI inside fabs: FabTwin virtual environment live** — TSMC is running NVIDIA Omniverse, CUDA-X, and Metropolis AI tools inside production fabs for process simulation, layout planning, and nanometer-scale defect inspection. AI is now eating its own supply chain — better yields, faster design iteration, eventually lower GPU unit costs. Directionally good for everyone on the waitlist.

🖥️ **Warner Bros. Discovery is rebuilding its ad-tech stack around agentic AI on AWS — phased rollout underway** — Some audience forecasting and measurement capabilities are already live in 2026; unified media planning is targeted for Q3, with order management, pricing, and campaign stewardship following in Q4. Not fully live yet, but this is the most ambitious Fortune 500 agentic ad-tech program on record — and WBD’s Q3 results will set the benchmark for media vertical RFPs in H2.

📊 **Claude Sonnet 4.8 expected before month end; 323+ AI model releases tracked in 2026** — The frontier model release cadence has become relentless: GPT-5, GPT-5.5 Instant, GPT-5.2, Gemini 3.5 Flash/Pro, and Claude iterations all landing within weeks of each other. Enterprise builders: model pinning and provider abstraction layers are now operational hygiene. Your stack needs to survive weekly model churn.

💡 **Wildcard — Cognizant Neuro AI now acts as control plane over ServiceNow-native agents** — The June 18 integration flips the architecture: Cognizant’s platform orchestrates ServiceNow agents alongside other enterprise systems rather than ServiceNow being the orchestrator. This “third-party control plane over platform-native agents” pattern is emerging as the dominant enterprise agentic architecture — and it’s where systems integrator margin is being locked in right now.
