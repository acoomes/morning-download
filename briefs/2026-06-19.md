## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🧠 **Fable 5 exits its free window on Monday — builders, update your cost models** — Anthropic's Fable 5 (the public-access Mythos-class model, released June 9) has been free on Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise plans through June 22; starting June 23 it requires usage credits; developers and startups who shipped production workloads during the free preview window need to stress-test unit economics before the billing clock starts.

🔮 **Google Gemini 3.5 Pro still in limited Vertex AI preview — GA launch imminent** — Unveiled at Google I/O (May 19) with a 2M-token context window and "Deep Think" reasoning mode, Gemini 3.5 Pro is not yet in the public Gemini app or AI Studio; when it goes GA (expected in the next 1-2 weeks), it will be the first direct benchmark competition with Fable 5 and reset the frontier pricing conversation.

🤖 **ChatGPT crosses 1 billion monthly active users** — OpenAI's flagship product has reached a milestone no software application has ever hit this fast; for enterprise builders, this matters less as an OpenAI story and more as a signal about ambient AI expectations — users now assume AI is everywhere, and products that don't meet that bar are losing by default.

📊 **NVIDIA RTX Spark "superchip" enters Dell/Lenovo laptops this fall — AMD and Intel drop on the news** — NVIDIA's new ARM-based chip (built with MediaTek, running Windows for ARM) will power AI PCs from Dell and Lenovo; AMD and Qualcomm shares fell as Wall Street priced the competitive threat; Jensen Huang is now competing for the client device layer, the data center layer, and everything in between.

🔌 **Anthropic scales Claude Mythos to critical infrastructure in 15+ countries** — Announced June 2, Anthropic is deploying its restricted Mythos tier to critical infrastructure operators globally, following the Japan financial consortium model; this is becoming a systematic enterprise go-to-market motion — sovereign AI deployment through consortium governance, not open APIs.

🛠️ **agnt8x (EightX Labs) opens public agent marketplace** — The platform (launched publicly June 8) allows teams to recruit, onboard, operate, and monetize AI agents through a builder marketplace, unified audit trail, and multi-agent conductor; early signal of an emerging "agent labor marketplace" category — the infrastructure layer between human operators and autonomous agents is starting to take shape as a product.

💡 **Wildcard: 79% of enterprises face AI implementation challenges despite near-universal adoption** — Research shows 97% of executives say they've deployed AI agents, but only 20% say their talent is highly prepared; 60% of C-suites are planning layoffs for non-adopters; the adoption-execution gap is the defining tension of enterprise AI right now, and it's not resolving on its own.
