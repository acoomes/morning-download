## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🔐 **Schwab and JPMorgan are blocking outside AI agents from moving customer money, and Schwab says it will let outside tools see balances only through controlled MCP servers** — Schwab CEO Rick Wurster told Bloomberg Television the firm is blocking unauthorized agents, including Meta's Muse, and wants clients to use its own assistant, Charley; he framed the problem as liability: "We can't control it, the client can't control it, something could go wrong and who's liable?" JPMorgan's Jamie Dimon warned on the October 13 earnings call about "rogue agents." American Banker notes a more cynical reading: agents that sweep idle cash into higher-yielding products threaten brokers' interest income. **For platform builders:** the delegated-credential pattern, where an agent logs in as the user, is closing at the largest financial institutions. Read-only access through MCP-style endpoints is the opening, so design agent integrations around scoped, revocable, per-user authorization.

Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/schwab-blocks-ai-agents-meta-muse) · [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/schwab-blocks-unauthorized-ai-agents-points-clients-to-charley)

🛡️ **Microsoft introduced Guardian Agents on October 19 to monitor the AI agents a company runs, and said frontier models "can't be trusted" on their own** — The feature is part of Project Perception, and Microsoft's announcement says discovery expanded by 35 types of local agents, for a total of 60 supported, including Claude Code, OpenAI Codex and GitHub Copilot. Critics note the commercial angle: Microsoft is selling an oversight layer that the model labs do not control. **For enterprise AI delivery:** expect security teams to ask for an independent agent-monitoring layer in every agent rollout, which makes audit logs and identity first-class requirements, not add-ons.

Sources: [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/10/19/agents-are-your-newest-insiders-security-must-move-at-their-speed/) · [Redmond Magazine](https://redmondmag.com/articles/2026/10/19/microsoft-expands-project-perception-to-address-security-risks-from-ai-agents.aspx) · [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/microsoft-admits-frontier-models-cant-be-trusted-unveils-new-security-tools-to-treat-ai-agents-as-insider-threats)

🎲 **Wildcard: Tab came out of stealth at a $300 million valuation with an AI agent that lives in iMessage and WhatsApp** — Users text requests such as booking flights, calling businesses and drafting emails, and spending requires approval. The company declined to say how much it raised, and coverage disagrees on whether the round was pre-seed or seed, so the valuation is the only firm number. It is in private beta behind a waitlist. The bank lockouts above are the obstacle this category runs into the moment an agent needs to touch money.

Sources: [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/tab-ai-assistant-300m-valuation-stealth/) · [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/160851-tab-exits-stealth-at-300m-valuation-in-crowded-ai-assistant-race/)

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** The undefined "built using DeepSeek technology" language in the DHS order. Why it matters: the order covers third-party products and services, the notice does not define the term, and the exception window is described inconsistently. Within the next 30 days, expect federal-facing vendors and their procurement teams to ask which models sit inside their stacks. If you sell agent platforms or consulting into civilian agencies, get a written model-provenance answer ready before a customer asks.

**One-Liner:** The Fed, Washington and the big banks all spent the week deciding how much to trust things they cannot fully see inside: rates, models and agents.
