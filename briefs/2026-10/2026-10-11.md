## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🔌 **OpenAI retires GPT-5.5 from ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work and Codex on Wednesday, October 14** — The retirement covers every plan tier, and the API is reportedly unaffected. Coverage points Codex users to GPT-5.6 Sol as the replacement. OpenAI has not published a cutoff time, and the notice reportedly promises no automatic remap. **For platform builders:** audit workspace defaults, saved model settings, custom agents, scheduled tasks and scripts for hard-coded `gpt-5.5` references today, not Wednesday afternoon.

Investors are paying for agent distribution before agent reliability is proven. Dealroom lists a different valuation, so the $10B figure rests on the majority of reports.

For European enterprises with sovereignty requirements, Mistral is now a funded alternative to the U.S. labs.
