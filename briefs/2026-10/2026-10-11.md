## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

📊 **The week ahead: banks report Tuesday, CPI lands Wednesday, and the S&P 500 sits near a record** — Reuters' week-ahead piece says JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, Citigroup and Wells Fargo report Tuesday, with Morgan Stanley and Bank of America on Wednesday. Strong earnings are priced in; a hot CPI print is the cheapest way to spoil the week.

The September minutes say most officials still expect another increase by year-end. September payrolls added only 29,000 jobs, and roic.ai and Admiral Markets report October-hike odds at roughly 17–25% on CME FedWatch, with Goldman moving its call from October to December. We covered the minutes on October 8. The new point is the split: policymakers want to hike, and the data is not cooperating. Expect volatility around Wednesday's CPI and the October 28 decision, not a clean signal.

Trump has said the U.S. will not attack Iran before the November 3 midterms. Tehran's stated conditions remain lifting the naval blockade, releasing frozen assets and ending the war across all fronts. I found no coverage of the reply itself, so the open question this week is whether Iran's answer moves Hormuz or just the rhetoric.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🔌 **OpenAI retires GPT-5.5 from ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work and Codex on Wednesday, October 14** — The retirement covers every plan tier, and the API is reportedly unaffected. Replacement guidance has shifted: September coverage named GPT-5.6 Sol, while OpenAI's current model documentation reportedly directs paid-plan Codex users to GPT-6 Sol and Free/Go users to GPT-6 Luna. I could not open the documentation, so confirm the target there before changing any settings. Regular ChatGPT conversations are reported to move to the new default automatically, but coverage does not say what happens to pinned `gpt-5.5` references in saved settings, scheduled tasks or scripts, and I found no published cutoff time. **For platform builders:** audit workspace defaults, saved model settings, custom agents, scheduled tasks and scripts for hard-coded `gpt-5.5` references today, not Wednesday afternoon.

🤖 **Instinct raised a $1B Series C at a $10B valuation for a consumer agent with 14 employees** — Announced September 28, with Sequoia, Benchmark and Coatue participating. The valuation is about four times the $2.5B set in its August 26 Series B, and the product is reportedly still in early access. It is a catch-up item: we had not covered the round. Investors are paying for agent distribution before agent reliability is proven. Dealroom lists a different valuation, so the $10B figure rests on the majority of reports.

💼 **Mistral's €3B Series D, led by Samsung, values it above €21B** — Announced September 8, co-led by EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund and PSG Equity, with BlackRock, Advent and Luxembourg joining. Mistral calls it Europe's largest tech equity round, a claim I could not independently verify. Another catch-up item. For European enterprises with sovereignty requirements, Mistral is now a funded alternative to the U.S. labs.

🧪 **Claude is credited with an algorithm that refutes the 3SUM and APSP hypotheses; independent review is the open question** — A preprint by Josh Alman and Virginia Vassilevska Williams, posted to arXiv on October 5, reports a truly subquadratic 3SUM algorithm and a truly subcubic APSP algorithm. Secondary coverage says the paper credits Claude with developing the core algorithm, and that Anthropic published a Lean formalization in its `formal-math` repository. I could not open the arXiv page or the repository directly, so exponents and the credit framing come from secondary write-ups. These hypotheses underpin a large body of complexity lower bounds, so if the result holds it matters well beyond AI. Wait for expert review before treating it as settled.

🔮 **Wildcard: Anthropic paused its new startup credits within days because applications swamped it** — Anthropic expanded Claude for Startups on October 6, then said it underestimated demand and paused the free Claude Team and $1,000 API-credit offers. Its own post cites hundreds of thousands of applicants, and it is re-reviewing applications. Already-claimed offers stay in accounts, and the partner offers and office hours continue; no end date for the pause has been published. Demand for subsidized frontier access is high enough that giveaways get throttled; budget for capacity limits, not only price.

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** The GPT-5.5 retirement on Wednesday. Model retirements rarely make headlines, but hard-coded model names in scheduled tasks, agent configs and CLI scripts can fail when the model they pin is retired, and coverage does not say how OpenAI handles pinned references or publish a cutoff time. Within three days, some teams may find broken automations. If you ship agentic systems to enterprises, pin model aliases in one config layer and run a deprecation check in CI.

**One-Liner:** A quiet Sunday before a loud week: banks, CPI and a model retirement are all due by Wednesday, and the weekend's one live thread is Iran's reply to the U.S. counterproposal.
