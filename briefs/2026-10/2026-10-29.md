## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

🏦 **The Fed held rates steady on Wednesday, October 28, in a 10–2 vote, with the two dissenters — Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack and Governor Lisa Cook — favoring a hike** — Stocks closed modestly higher: the S&P 500 rose 0.28% to 7,743.98, the Nasdaq gained 0.27% to 26,549.85 and the Dow finished at 52,475.10. Markets now price a December 9 hike at roughly 83–85%, depending on the source. The hold was the easy part; two hawkish dissents turn December into the live decision, and a rate-sensitive enterprise software budget should be planned against higher-for-longer, not a cut.

Part of Google Cloud's growth came from TPU system sales. The lesson for anyone selling AI infrastructure or services: investors are now paying for growth that comes with cash discipline and punishing growth that burns it. ## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🧠 **Anthropic's IPO debate moved from the filing to the valuation: coverage this week describes investors split between "speculative fiction" and a "generational moment"** — The S-1, covered in earlier briefings, shows $16.3 billion of first-half 2026 revenue against $4.6 billion for all of 2025, and CNBC reports an $80.7 billion first-half net loss, most of it non-operating (about $72 billion of accounting charges tied to convertible notes, as covered on October 27), so it is not a read on delivery economics; the valuation range and a possible 15–20% retail allocation come from press reports, not the filing. **For enterprise AI delivery:** a public price on a frontier lab will become the benchmark your CFO uses on every model contract, which makes the operating line, not the headline net loss, the number to watch in the filing.

Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/27/-anthropic-ipo-investors-growth-losses-s-1.html) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/live/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-frontier-ai-lab-discloses-revenue-and-risks-in-highly-anticipated-s-1-filing-141702607.html)

🤖 **GitHub used Universe on October 28 to pitch a software delivery system built around agents: a new Copilot app that runs parallel agent sessions across repositories, and a generally available "Fusion" routing feature that picks models per task** — GitHub claims Fusion Max comes within two points of Claude Opus 5.5 Max on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index at about half the cost; that is GitHub's own benchmark claim, and this item rests on one aggregator digest, so verify against GitHub's announcement. **For platform builders:** multi-model routing and cross-surface session continuity are becoming table stakes in coding-agent products. If you orchestrate agents, expect customers to ask why you aren't doing both.

Sources: [DEV Community](https://dev.to/hiroki-ii-ai/ai-daily-digest-october-29-2026-github-rebuilds-the-sdlc-around-agents-chipmakers-buy-into-1m05)

🔌 **Google added VPC connectivity and static-IP egress for custom MCP servers in Gemini Enterprise on October 27, routing outbound tool traffic through Agent Gateway** — Private MCP servers can now sit inside a customer's VPC, supported in us-central1 and europe-west1. **For enterprise agent delivery:** "where does the tool call travel" is the question security reviewers ask first, and a major platform answering it with network controls sets the bar other vendors will be held to.

Sources: [Google Cloud](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/release-notes)

🔧 **MCP is turning into a governance product category: Celigo launched MCP tool management on October 20, Quantum Metric made its MCP server generally available on October 22 with 46 tools, and Interact exposed its intranet capabilities through MCP on October 26** — Each works with the major assistants, and Interact says it uses existing user permissions. **For builders:** the interesting part is not another connector, it is that vendors are shipping the permissioning and tool-management layer around MCP. Plan for per-tool access policy as a first-class feature.

Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/20/celigo-launches-mcp-tool-management-for-enterprise-ai-assistants-and-agent-access/) · [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/22/3386403/0/en/quantum-metric-mcp-gives-enterprise-ai-agents-the-experience-context-to-act-on-what-matters-most.html) · [Manila Times](https://manilatimes.net/2026/10/26/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/interact-brings-700-intranet-capabilities-to-enterprise-ai-assistants-with-interact-mcp/2454191)

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** The MCP governance layer. Why it matters: Google putting private MCP servers inside customer VPCs while Celigo and others ship tool-management products means agent security review is shifting from "which model" to "which tools can this agent call, from where, with whose permissions." Within 30 days, expect enterprise procurement checklists and RFPs to ask for exactly that, and expect agent platforms without per-tool policy and network isolation to stall in review.

**One-Liner:** The Fed held but sounded ready to hike, and Wall Street spent the day deciding which AI spenders have earned the right to keep spending.
