For enterprise-software readers, the relevance is indirect: energy-cost pass-through is still the macro variable behind IT budgets.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🧠 **Anthropic publicly filed its S-1 on October 26, applying to list on Nasdaq under the symbol "ANT"; reported figures are $16.3 billion of first-half 2026 revenue and an $80.7 billion net loss** — The loss is mostly non-operating: a Wall Street Journal-based report says about $72 billion was accounting charges tied to convertible notes. The filing sets no price or share count. The earlier confidential draft showed 47% of 2025 revenue flowing through cloud marketplaces whose operators are also Anthropic's investors, compute suppliers and competitors, and reports say roughly a quarter of first-half revenue came from two customers. Reports on the draft disagree on the period and on the compute-commitment total, so check the filing itself before quoting any of them. **For enterprise AI delivery:** this is the first audited look at a frontier lab's economics. If Claude is in your stack, the risk-factor section is now your vendor-risk reading, and procurement will ask about concentration and compute dependence.

Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/26/anthropics-s-1/) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/live/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-frontier-ai-lab-discloses-revenue-and-risks-in-highly-anticipated-s-1-filing-141702607.html) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/anthropic-s-1-shows-163b-h1-revenue-25-from-two-customers-and-807b-non-cash) · [Newsquawk](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/anthropic-saw-usd-163bln-in-h1-26-prev-usd-11bln-a-year-earlier-and-posted-an-usd-807bln-net-loss-driven-largely-by-usd-72bln-in-accounting-charges-tied-to-convertible-notes-according-to-a-draft-ipo-prospectus-viewed-by-wsj) · [Khaleej Times (Reuters)](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/anthropic-ipo-prospectus-lays-bare-deep-dependence-on-big-tech-partners)

💾 **Chip substrates, not chips, look like the tightest link in the AI supply chain: TrendForce, as reported October 26–27, put ABF substrate lead times at 48 to 56 weeks against about 12 in a balanced market** — Nvidia and AMD are reported to be moving to lock up substrate, packaging and test capacity. Leading-edge silicon can be ordered and still sit idle behind an unglamorous component. **For platform builders:** capacity planning for GPU-backed agent infrastructure should assume delivery slips set by packaging, not by the fab.

Sources: [DIGITIMES](https://digitimes.com/news/a20261027PD218/amd-nvidia-capacity-substrate-expansion.html)

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** The ABF substrate squeeze. Why it matters: 48-to-56-week lead times mean capacity ordered now lands in late 2027, so any lab or cloud that has not already locked supply competes for what is left. Expect capacity-reservation announcements and pricing pressure to show up in the next round of Nvidia and AMD commentary, and expect GPU delivery dates quoted to you to carry more slack.

**One-Liner:** Anthropic opened its books, the Fed is about to speak, and the scarcest thing in AI turns out to be a circuit-board substrate.
