## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** Applied Materials deepening its Besi stake. Why it matters: the AI build-out is limited by packaging and substrates as much as by chips, and a toolmaker locking in its tie to the leading packaging-equipment vendor, pending an antitrust waiting period, suggests that constraint lasts well into 2027. Within 30 days, watch whether the Hart-Scott-Rodino period clears and whether the rumored CoWoS price increase is confirmed; either one reprices GPU capacity for anyone budgeting agent infrastructure.

**One-Liner:** Wall Street bought a single AI headline on Tuesday and now waits on the Fed to say whether it can afford to keep buying.
