# Morning Download — April 29, 2026

## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

- **🛢️ Iran War, Day 60: Hormuz stalemate deepens as Trump rejects latest ceasefire offer.** Iran proposed reopening the Strait while deferring nuclear talks — Trump called it unlikely to fly; Brent crude closed at $111.26, WTI at $99.93 (+3%). ~20% of global oil and LNG transits the Strait, and every day it stays shut compounds the inflation shock the IMF now calls the worst energy price surge since Russia's 2022 invasion.

- **🏳️ UAE quits OPEC after 57 years — cartel loses its second-largest producer.** Effective May 1, the UAE is walking out to pump freely, since its Hormuz-bound exports are blockaded anyway. OPEC losing a founding Gulf member mid-crisis is a structural blow to Saudi Arabia's market-management grip; watch for Kuwait or Iraq reconsidering membership next.

- **🏦 Fed holds rates at 3.5–3.75%; Powell takes his final bow.** Third consecutive pause, unanimous and fully priced in. The real story: this is almost certainly Jerome Powell's last press conference as Chair — Kevin Warsh takes over in June. Markets get a lame-duck Fed precisely when geopolitical risk is peaking. Consumer confidence beat estimates (92.8 vs. 89.8), which is the one bright spot.

- **📊 Four Mag 7 companies report tonight — $500B+ in AI capex on trial.** Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft all drop earnings after close. Combined 2026 AI infrastructure commitments: Meta $115–135B, Alphabet $175–185B, Amazon ~$200B. The question every investor is asking: where's the revenue? If cloud growth doesn't accelerate to match the spend, the AI trade narrative cracks.

- **🇨🇺 Senate clears path for Trump military action against Cuba, 51–47.** Republicans blocked a resolution requiring congressional approval before any Cuba strike. Pentagon is reportedly drafting plans; Havana says it's "ready to fight." This is the most serious US-Cuba tension in decades — and it's mostly buried under Iran headlines.

- **🌋 Wildcard: AI reconstructs the face of a Pompeii victim for the first time.** Archaeologists used AI to digitally reconstruct the face of a man killed in the 79 AD Vesuvius eruption. Mostly a curiosity today, but a preview of what AI-assisted archaeology does to history — we're about to "meet" a lot of people who died centuries ago.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

- **🔒 Claude Mythos locked behind 50-company Project Glasswing consortium — and the benchmarks are alarming.** Anthropic's most capable model ever (97.6% on USAMO 2026, 93.9% on SWE-bench Verified, reportedly 271 Firefox zero-days) went straight into a closed partner program, not a public API. Launch partners: Amazon, Apple, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Microsoft, Palo Alto Networks. If you're building enterprise security or code analysis tooling, the capability ceiling just moved dramatically — and it's access-controlled. This is Anthropic making a deliberate bet that frontier AI in offense-capable domains requires managed access.

- **💰 Google commits $40B to Anthropic; new Broadcom TPU deal for 2027 production.** The investment (announced April 24) is the largest single AI investment since Microsoft's OpenAI bets. Separately, Anthropic signed a multi-gigawatt TPU capacity deal with Google and Broadcom starting 2027 — a structural hedge against NVIDIA pricing power that most Anthropic competitors don't have.

- **📡 MCP hits critical mass: 10,000 public servers, 97M monthly SDK downloads.** The Model Context Protocol — donated to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation in December — now has all major providers integrated and 1,200 attendees at this month's NYC Dev Summit. MCP is quietly becoming the USB-C of agentic AI. If you're building an orchestration stack without MCP-native support, you're building technical debt.

- **🏢 Enterprise agents: 96% deployed, only 10% scaled — sprawl is the real crisis.** OutSystems research finds virtually every org runs AI agents, but only 1 in 10 has scaled beyond pilots; 94% cite agent sprawl as a major risk vector. The gap between "we have agents" and "agents are under governance" is the primary enterprise AI delivery problem right now.

- **⚡ NVIDIA posts $68B in Q4 revenue (+73% YoY); TSMC unveils A13/N2U nodes for 2029.** NVIDIA Data Center alone hit $62.3B (+75%). TSMC debuted its next-generation process nodes while cautioning shortages may persist past 2027. AI now drives nearly a third of TSMC's business. The supply/demand gap in leading-edge silicon is not closing — it's extending to the end of the decade.

- **🤖 Adobe launches CX Enterprise: full agentic customer lifecycle management.** Adobe's new system handles acquisition through retention via AI agents — a signal that legacy creative/marketing platforms are repositioning entirely around agentic orchestration. Watch Adobe as a bellwether for how category leaders absorb agentic AI without looking like they built it themselves.

- **🔬 Wildcard: Nature study finds human scientists still outperform the best AI agents on complex tasks.** Published this week, the study found frontier AI falls behind humans on genuinely open-ended research problems. A useful counterweight to the "AI replaces scientists" narrative — and an honest calibration of where autonomous agent capability actually stands heading into 2026 H2.

## Closing

**Sleeper Story:** MCP's governance moment is coming. The protocol just hit 10K public servers and 97M downloads while its Linux Foundation stewardship is still brand new. Within 30 days, expect the first high-profile enterprise security incidents traced to misconfigured MCP servers — which will accelerate the governance and credentialing conversation around agent infrastructure faster than any roadmap announcement.

**One-Liner:** Big Tech bets a half-trillion dollars on AI's ROI tonight while the world's most important oil chokepoint stays shut, Powell exits stage left, and Anthropic's best model sits locked in a vault — today tells us a lot about whether the past three years' bets were right.
