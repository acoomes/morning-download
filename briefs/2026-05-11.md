## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

- 🛡️ **Anthropic's Project Glasswing + Claude Mythos Preview: AI autonomously finds and exploits zero-days at scale.** Claude Mythos — shared with select partners (AWS, Apple, Cisco, Google, JPMorgan, Microsoft) — independently discovered thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities across major OSes and browsers, and autonomously exploited a 17-year-old FreeBSD RCE with no human in the loop. Anthropic isn't making it generally available, and that decision tells you everything. This is the frontier benchmark for autonomous code agents — and it's simultaneously impressive and alarming. Directly relevant: this is what your agentic platform's security posture needs to be modeled against.

- 🤖 **OpenAI launches GPT-5.5 and Workspace Agents — the successor to custom GPTs for enterprise.** GPT-5.5 ships as the new ChatGPT default with stronger multi-step reasoning and tool use. Workspace Agents replace custom GPTs with enterprise-grade agents that plug into Slack, Salesforce, and more; a new admin console adds governance and analytics. The race to become the enterprise AI operating system is fully on.

- 🌐 **Google consolidates its entire AI stack: Vertex AI → Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform at Cloud Next 2026.** Google absorbed Agentspace and unified its offering under Gemini Enterprise, while pushing the Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol hard. Most people are watching the branding; the real move is Google betting on owning the multi-agent coordination layer — the infrastructure play nobody's writing about enough.

- 🏗️ **Microsoft launches Agent 365 at $15/user/month: a dedicated governance and control plane for enterprise AI agents.** This is purpose-built infrastructure for managing, auditing, and governing agents at scale across the enterprise. The pricing signals this becomes standard line-item infrastructure. If you're deploying agents into enterprise environments, this is the governance pattern to watch and potentially compete with.

- 🔌 **MCP crosses 97M installs; enterprise-grade features (OAuth 2.1, gateways, audit trails) landing in 2026 roadmap.** The Model Context Protocol is crossing the enterprise chasm: stateless horizontal scaling, SSO-integrated auth, formal audit support, and MCP Gateway behavior are all in the 2026 roadmap. 10,000+ public servers live; Stripe, Vercel, and Fortune 500 data platforms are early adopters. MCP is becoming the integration standard — build MCP-native or plan for refactoring later.

- 💾 **TSMC fully booked through 2028; NVIDIA Vera Rubin (3.5x training, 5x inference vs. Blackwell) in full production.** Vera Rubin runs on TSMC 3nm. Advanced node capacity (N2, N3, A14 roadmap) is entirely reserved through 2028. Supply is still the binding constraint on AI infrastructure — plan for constrained GPU availability through the end of the decade.

- 💸 **AI pricing floor collapses: Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite at $0.25/M tokens; GLM-4.7 at $0.11/M.** Commodity-tier capable models are now essentially free compared to 18 months ago. Differentiation is migrating to the agent layer — governance, integrations, reliability, enterprise trust. Pure model plays without enterprise wrappers are getting squeezed into irrelevance.
