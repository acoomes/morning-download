Anthropic's ARR has crossed $30B (up from $9B at end of 2025). Two of the world's biggest cloud providers are now deeply invested in their own competitor.

A genuine political earthquake in the EU's eastern flank — watch for Hungary's NATO and Brussels positioning to shift.

Timescale is millions of years, but the finding is a reminder that "permanent" is always relative.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

- 🤖 **DeepSeek V4 preview lands: 1.6T-parameter flagship, 1M-token context, Huawei-native stack.** V4-Pro (1.6T params, 49B activated) and V4-Flash are live on the API with a new Hybrid Attention Architecture for long-context tasks. Buried lede: the full launch is delayed because DeepSeek is rebuilding its software stack on Huawei Ascend chips — China is quietly building an AI compute stack that doesn't need NVIDIA. This matters more than any benchmark. 🚩 *Critical for anyone tracking AI geopolitics and open-source frontier models.*

- 🏗️ **MCP transferred to open-source Agentic AI Foundation; Databricks ships enterprise governance layer.** Anthropic handed MCP to a community-led foundation with OpenAI, Google, AWS, Microsoft, and Cloudflare all signing on. Databricks simultaneously shipped AI Gateway support for MCP-based agent permissions — controlling which agents can access which external systems with fine-grained controls. This is the infrastructure moment that makes production agentic systems actually governable. 🚩 *Directly relevant to anyone building enterprise agent platforms.*

- 🖥️ **Microsoft makes Copilot agentic actions GA across Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.** Multi-step, app-native agent actions are out of preview and live in the full Office suite. Hundreds of millions of enterprise users are now getting their first real agentic workflow experience — the normalization of AI agents as default tooling has begun.

- 🏢 **Salesforce "Headless 360" exposes entire platform as API/MCP/CLI for AI agents.** Every Salesforce capability (CRM, service, marketing, commerce) is now callable by agents like Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex without opening a browser. Google Workspace Studio (April 22) similarly lets business users build no-code agents across Gmail, Docs, and Sheets. The enterprise SaaS layer is becoming an agent-addressable API surface.

- 📡 **TSMC Q1 2026: revenue +35% YoY, net income +58%; AI projected to drive a third of business.** Blowout numbers confirm AI compute demand shows no sign of slowing. A13 and N2U process nodes targeted for 2029 production, new Arizona packaging facility planned. TSMC is warning chip shortages could persist beyond 2027 given demand and hardware costs.

- 🧬 **AiChemy multi-agent system deployed for drug discovery at scale.** A new orchestration platform coordinates multiple AI agents scanning millions of medical papers and databases for drug candidates. Early signal of how coordinated agent networks will reshape scientific research — and a model for high-value agentic deployments beyond software engineering.

- ⚠️ **Only 11–14% of enterprise AI agent pilots reach production at scale.** The uncomfortable stat buried in April's enterprise AI playbooks: 86–89% of agent pilots fail to generate durable value. The bottleneck is governance and change management, not model capability. If you're building or selling enterprise agent infrastructure, this gap is your wedge.

## Sleeper Story

**DeepSeek's pivot to Huawei Ascend chips.** The V4 launch delay is less about model quality and more about China methodically building a fully domestic AI stack independent of Western semiconductors. If Ascend achieves competitive performance at scale — which DeepSeek's engineering track record makes credible — it rewrites the geopolitical calculus of the AI chip race within the next 6–12 months. The U.S. export control regime becomes strategically moot if China ships a viable alternative at frontier quality.

## One-Liner

*The AI arms race is eating itself: Google just committed $40B to a company it's actively competing against, while the compute infrastructure powering the whole thing is quietly being rebuilt in China without NVIDIA.*
