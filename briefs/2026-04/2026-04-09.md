## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

- 🦙 **Meta launches Muse Spark — first closed proprietary model from Muse Superintelligence Labs, with native multi-agent "contemplating mode."** Natively multimodal, features visual chain-of-thought and multi-agent coordination; powers Meta AI app, smart glasses, and the entire Facebook/Instagram/WhatsApp stack. Meta breaking its open-source tradition signals the frontier is too competitive to gift away — and Alexandr Wang's $14B bet is now live in production.

- 🔐 **Anthropic's Claude Mythos withheld from public release on cybersecurity grounds — restricted to select partners under Project Glasswing.** First time a major lab has voluntarily suppressed a flagship model citing security risks. Sets a precedent for capability-gated releases; if you're not a Glasswing partner, you don't have access to the current capability ceiling.

- 🏭 **Global AI deploys agentic platform at a Fortune 500 pharma across regulatory reporting and payroll; 74% of businesses planning agentic deployment (Trent AI, €11M seed).** Real production deployments in regulated industries are live. The gap between "planning agentic AI" (74%) and "fully agentic" (6% per Deloitte) is the commercial opportunity — and pharma's regulatory workflows are the proof-of-concept that matters most for enterprise risk tolerance.

- 🔌 **MCP 2026 roadmap: Streamable HTTP becomes the standard transport; MCP servers evolving to act as autonomous agents negotiating with other MCP servers.** The protocol is maturing from "tool connector" to "agent-to-agent communication layer." MCP v2.1 now supported natively by Claude Desktop 3.2.1 and Cursor 2.5.0. Build against it or build tech debt.

- 🧠 **Google's Gemma 4 drops under Apache 2.0; Gemini 3.1 Pro leads 13/16 major benchmarks; Claude Sonnet 4.6 leads GDPval-AA Elo for real expert-level work.** Open-weight models are closing the gap fast — Gemma 4 emphasizes coding, agentic AI, and multimodal reasoning. The enterprise model selection calculus is shifting from "which closed model" to "closed vs. fine-tuned open."

- ⚡ **NVIDIA set to surpass Apple as TSMC's #1 customer; Samsung counters with $73B 2026 capex vs. TSMC's ~$50B; Rubin Ultra production starts H2 2026.** Nvidia's annual chip cadence means continuous enterprise GPU refresh cycles. Hard GPU supply constraint through 2028 is still the binding bottleneck — plan for constrained compute availability in any agentic infrastructure roadmap.

- 🏥 **Wildcard: Utah becomes the first US state to grant AI authority to autonomously renew drug prescriptions — a live test of AI in direct patient care.** If the 30-day outcomes data looks good, expect rapid national policy debate. Healthcare is the highest-stakes regulated vertical for agentic AI — watch this one closely.

**Sleeper Story:** Iran's foreign minister explicitly framing the choice as "ceasefire OR war via Israel" puts Washington in a structural bind. Israel is not party to the ceasefire and has no incentive to stop Lebanon strikes. If Iran uses the next Israeli strike as a Hormuz pretext, oil goes back above $100 before US markets open Monday, the Fed's one-cut projection evaporates, and the relief rally fully unwinds. The two-week clock started ticking yesterday.
