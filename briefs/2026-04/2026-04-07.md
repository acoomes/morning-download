## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

- **NVIDIA Vera Rubin enters full production — 10x inference cost reduction vs. Blackwell.** Seven-chip platform (Vera CPU, Rubin GPU, NVLink 6, ConnectX-9, BlueField-4, Spectrum-6 + Groq LPU co-processor) shipping H2 2026. AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, OCI are first deployers. 10x cheaper per-token inference + 4x fewer GPUs to train MoE models will materially shift enterprise AI economics.

- **Claude Mythos (codename: Capybara) leaked — Anthropic's above-Opus model in limited early access.** Accidental infra leak March 26 exposed internal docs describing it as "by far the most powerful AI model we have ever developed." No public API, no pricing, no launch date. Polymarket gives ~25% odds of public release before April 30. If it ships before DeepSeek V4, the frontier leaderboard reshuffles immediately.

- **DeepSeek V4 expected late April — 1T parameter MoE running on Huawei Ascend 950PR chips.** Reuters confirmed April 4 that V4 runs on Huawei silicon, zero NVIDIA. 37B active params, 1M context, native multimodal, expected ~$0.30/MTok. Most geopolitically significant AI story of the month: China validating a fully parallel AI semiconductor stack outside US export controls.

- **MCP crosses 97M monthly installs — formally an industry standard under the Linux Foundation.** Anthropic's Model Context Protocol, donated to the Agentic AI Foundation in December 2025, is now integrated by all major cloud providers. Gartner projects 40% of enterprise apps will embed task-specific agents by end of 2026 (vs <5% today). If you're building agentic platforms, the interoperability bet is settled — MCP is the USB-C layer.

- **Frontier model standings: GPT-5.4 for computer-use, Claude Sonnet 4.6 for agentic work, Gemini 3.1 Pro for reasoning.** GPT-5.4: 83% GDPVal, leads computer-use. Claude Sonnet 4.6: 1,633 GDPVal-AA Elo, leads sustained agentic workflows. Gemini 3.1 Pro: 94.3% GPQA Diamond, cheapest output at $2/MTok. No universal winner — pick by use case.

- **NVIDIA launches Physical AI roadmap at National Robotics Week — Cosmos and GR00T in production.** New Cosmos world-foundation models and GR00T open humanoid robotics models, Isaac Lab-Arena evaluation environments, and Jetson Thor edge platform. Physical AI transitioning from R&D to early production deployments. Agentic platform timelines for embodied AI just got shorter.

- **Wildcard: Neuro-symbolic AI research claims 100x energy reduction over pure neural networks.** Academic results showing neuro-symbolic approaches cut energy use by 100x while improving accuracy on structured tasks. Pre-production — but if this holds at scale, it challenges the "more compute always wins" assumption driving current infrastructure capex.
