## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

🇮🇷 **Iran-US ceasefire collapses — airstrikes resume, Strait of Hormuz re-escalates** — New US airstrikes against Iran launched Thursday as the week's fragile ceasefire disintegrated; Tehran responded with strikes targeting US-allied positions across the region. Qatar is now mediating back-channel talks. Oil snapped higher as markets re-priced Strait of Hormuz closure risk — Brent near $72 — a reminder that the "peace dividend" embedded in crude prices over the past two weeks can unwind in hours.

🏛️ **NATO Turkey summit closes: Trump meets Zelenskyy, grants Ukraine Patriot manufacturing license** — As the Ankara summit concluded Friday, Trump met Zelenskyy and extended a license for Ukraine to produce Patriot air defense systems domestically. This is a strategically meaningful shift: moving from "shipping Ukraine weapons" to "enabling Ukraine to build them" changes the long-term attrition calculus and reduces dependence on US appropriations for every replacement system.

📊 **SK Hynix raises $26.5B in the biggest foreign US IPO in history — jumps 13% on debut** — The South Korean memory chip leader priced ADRs at $149, demand ran 7x oversubscribed, and it closed its first Nasdaq trading day (Friday) up 13% — marking the largest first-time listing by a foreign company in the US. As NVIDIA's primary HBM3E supplier, this offering is less a traditional capital raise and more a leveraged bet on AI memory demand. The US government is already pressing the company to build new domestic fabs.

💰 **Fed holds at 3.5-3.75%; Warsh abandons forward guidance entirely** — Nine FOMC officials now project at least one rate hike before year-end. New Chair Kevin Warsh has scrapped traditional forward guidance in favor of pure data dependence — which means June CPI (dropping Monday) now moves markets more than any Fed statement. Inflation is running 4.2% YoY; any upside surprise will be felt acutely in rate-sensitive tech names.

💼 **M&A double-header: Kroger acquires Giant Eagle; Prologis pushes $16.9B Segro bid** — Kroger's Giant Eagle deal continues US grocery consolidation post-antitrust detente. The Prologis/Segro play is the more consequential structural story: it would create one of the world's largest industrial real estate operators at precisely the moment AI-driven logistics and data center demand is compressing warehouse and land supply across North America and Europe.

🕌 **Wildcard: The succession dynamic driving Iran's escalation — Mashhad fills for Khamenei's burial** — Enormous crowds packed Iran's holy city of Mashhad for the burial of Supreme Leader Khamenei. The political significance is strategic, not ceremonial: his hardline IRGC-aligned successor now controls Iran's nuclear and military posture, which explains why Tehran is simultaneously striking US allies *and* permitting Qatar to run back-channel ceasefire talks. The succession story is getting almost no coverage despite being the real driver of the conflict's unpredictability.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🧠 **Anthropic launches Claude Sonnet 5 — most agentic model yet, Mythos 5 and Fable 5 restrictions lifted** — Sonnet 5 delivers near-Opus 4.8 performance with native browser and terminal tool use, priced at Sonnet-tier rates. Simultaneously, the US lifted the export-control restrictions on Mythos 5 and Fable 5 that had been in place since June. **For enterprise builders:** Sonnet 5 is the new baseline for agentic workflows — the compute budget that previously required Opus now delivers comparable autonomous task completion at significantly lower cost.

🚀 **OpenAI's GPT-5.6 family (Sol/Terra/Luna) goes GA as ChatGPT's new default** — The three-tier family is live, though broad public access remains limited to roughly 20 government-vetted partner organizations, with general availability expected mid-to-late July. The technical detail worth tracking: OpenAI is running Sol on Cerebras wafer-scale hardware at up to 750 tokens/second — approximately 15x current GPU-based inference throughput. When that scales, the latency and throughput constraints on real-time agentic applications change structurally.

🏭 **Three humanoid robot companies race toward public markets in a single week** — Agility Robotics filed for a SPAC at a $2.5B valuation; Unitree cleared its Shanghai STAR Market IPO (valued above $14.7B); Tesla converted its former Fremont Model S production line for Optimus Gen3, targeting 100-150 units per week this month. The "humanoid robots are vaporware" narrative is officially stale — the question is now which supply chain can scale first.

🔐 **White House in final talks with OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic on 30-day frontier model pre-release review** — An announcement is expected this coming week: a voluntary framework giving the US government up to 30 days to review national security implications of new frontier models before public release. The sticking point is capability thresholds — labs want a high bar so only genuinely novel systems trigger review; government officials want lower thresholds to capture incremental capability gains. This is the most consequential US AI governance action since Biden's July 2023 voluntary commitments.

🌐 **Chinese AI now captures 45% of OpenRouter developer traffic — US share collapsed from 70% to 30% in a year** — DeepSeek commands 16.3% of all platform tokens; Xiaomi holds 21.1%, three times OpenAI's 7.5%. Chinese models are running 60-90% cheaper per token than equivalent US offerings. **For agentic platform builders:** if your cost model and geopolitical risk assessment don't explicitly account for Chinese LLM options, you're missing the largest price-performance shift in the market.

📊 **TSMC hiking prices 5-10% across all advanced nodes — ripples across the entire AI chip stack** — Advanced nodes represent 74% of TSMC's wafer revenue, meaning this is a broad industry cost increase, not a targeted adjustment. The hike affects Apple, NVIDIA, AMD, Qualcomm, Broadcom, and MediaTek. Most AI infrastructure cost projections and hyperscaler capex models don't have this headwind embedded — watch for revised guidance over the next two earnings cycles.

🤖 **Wildcard: Salesforce hits $800M Agentforce ARR (+169% YoY) — enterprise agentic AI is past "pilot"** — 18,500+ customers have adopted Agentforce as of Q4 fiscal 2026. Gartner separately projects 40% of enterprise applications will feature task-specific AI agents by year-end, up from under 5% in 2025. The platform layer is consolidating faster than expected. If your core enterprise workflow software doesn't have a native agent layer yet, the integration debt you're accumulating is about to become visible in procurement discussions.
