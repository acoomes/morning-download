## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

- 🚀 **OpenAI releases GPT-5.5 — a fully retrained agentic model scoring 82.7% on Terminal-Bench 2.0 and 58.6% on SWE-Bench Pro.** Six weeks after GPT-5.4, this is the first fully retrained base model since GPT-4.5. It's purpose-built for autonomous operation: web browsing, code writing, script execution, and cross-tool orchestration in a single pass. Rolling out now to Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise in ChatGPT and Codex. If you're building agentic coding pipelines, this just moved your capability ceiling — benchmark it today.

- 🐉 **DeepSeek V4 drops today: 1.6T parameter open-source MoE, MIT license, 1M context, trained on 33T tokens.** V4-Pro requires only 27% of single-token inference FLOPs vs. V3.2; API pricing undercuts US frontier models by 50-80%. Available on HuggingFace now. On the same day GPT-5.5 launched at OpenAI's full pricing, China released a competitive open-weight model for roughly $5M in training cost. Any enterprise paying full frontier rates should benchmark this before their next renewal conversation.

- ☁️ **Google Cloud commits $750M to partners for agentic AI development; announces $1B Merck partnership for R&D and manufacturing agents.** The $750M fund covers prototyping, agent building, upskilling, and embedded Google forward-deployed engineers across its 120,000-member partner ecosystem. Merck deploys across R&D, manufacturing, commercial, and corporate. Google is explicitly buying ecosystem lock-in for the agentic era. The big consulting and SI firms now have a race to become the dominant agentic delivery partner — and Google is paying their training costs.

- 💰 **Agentic AI funding up 142.6% YoY in 2026 through April; $2.66B raised across 44 rounds — AppZen lands $180M with Amazon and Salesforce as customers.** Replit leads the sector at $922M total funding. Notably, investor focus has explicitly shifted from raw model capability toward orchestration reliability, governance, and production-grade workflow management. The PoC era is over. Investors are funding the infrastructure layer that makes agents work in real enterprise environments.

- ⚡ **Intel surges 19% as CPU demand for agentic workloads drives its sixth straight earnings beat.** Data center revenue +22% to $5.1B; Google committed multi-gen Intel CPU use for AI; Intel joining Terafab Austin to fabricate chips for SpaceX, xAI, and Tesla using the forthcoming 14A process. The GPU-only AI compute thesis is quietly cracking. Agentic orchestration is CPU-heavy by nature — Intel's revival is structurally tied to the agentic shift, not just a legacy rebound.

- 🔒 **Trump administration vows to crack down on foreign exploitation of US AI models, explicitly singling out China.** Policy arrives the same day DeepSeek V4 launches with MIT-licensed weights that definitively close the gap with US frontier models at a fraction of the cost. This is the contradiction the administration has to solve: restrict access to US AI to slow China down, while China is demonstrating it may no longer need that access.

- 🛠️ **MCP Dev Summit North America in NYC drew 1,200 developers; ecosystem now 10,000+ active public servers and 97M monthly SDK downloads.** Chrome DevTools shipped native MCP integration this week. Every major AI provider is MCP-native. The ecosystem is past critical mass — MCP is now the interoperability layer for agentic systems, full stop. If you're still treating it as experimental infrastructure, you're building tech debt.

Watch for fab progress announcements in the next 30 days.
