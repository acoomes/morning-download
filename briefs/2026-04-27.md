Model capital costs as "higher for longer" through year-end.

- 🏗️ **QXO acquires TopBuild for $17B; SoftBank closes DigitalBridge** — Two significant deals close out April: QXO absorbs building materials distributor TopBuild in one of the month's biggest M&As, and DigitalBridge's acquisition by SoftBank clears at $16/share — infrastructure capital continues rotating into AI-adjacent assets.

- 🏃 **Wildcard: Sub-2-hour marathon — in a real race, for real** — Kenya's Sabastian Sawe ran 1:59:30 to win the London Marathon, the first-ever sub-2-hour result in a competitive race (Kipchoge's 2019 effort was a controlled trial). Human performance limits are being quietly rewritten while everyone watches Capitol Hill.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

- 🔐 **Anthropic's Claude Mythos: too dangerous to ship publicly** — Anthropic confirmed its most capable model ever exists and will not be publicly released. Fifty vetted organizations get gated access under "Project Glasswing" to use it defensively — scanning their own infrastructure for vulnerabilities before attackers can. **Builder relevance:** Anthropic is explicitly managing dual-use risk at the frontier; tiered capability access is becoming an industry norm, not an exception. Expect procurement conversations to evolve accordingly.

- 🚀 **OpenAI ships GPT-5.5, eyes unified AI "super app"** — GPT-5.5 reclaims top intelligence benchmarks and is positioned as a step toward a single interface merging ChatGPT, Codex-style coding, and browser capabilities. The race to own the AI operating system layer is accelerating fast.

- 🧠 **Google Gemini 3.1 Pro tops reasoning + ships enterprise agent infra** — Gemini 3.1 Pro hits 94.3% on GPQA Diamond, while Google simultaneously released new Workspace agent orchestration tools (dedicated agent inboxes, cross-tool automation, progress tracking). They're competing on model capability and deployment infrastructure at the same time.

- 🤖 **Meta enters frontier model competition with Muse Spark** — Meta Superintelligence Labs (the Alexandr Wang acquisition) shipped its first frontier model, code-named "Avocado." This is Meta's opening bet it can compete at the frontier after years of ceding ground on the leading edge.

- 💰 **Google Cloud commits $750M to partner agentic AI; Salesforce exposes everything as MCP** — Google is funding 120,000 partners to drive joint agentic deployments. Salesforce's Headless 360 (from TDX in April) exposes every CRM capability as an API, MCP tool, or CLI command so agents like Claude Code and Cursor can operate the platform without a browser. **The enterprise channel is explicitly redesigning its APIs for agent consumption.**

- 🔬 **TSMC Q1: +35% revenue, CoWoS packaging +80% YoY; shortages through 2027+** — AI chip demand is unambiguous. TSMC debuted A13/N2U process nodes targeting 2029 production and warned chip shortages could extend past 2027. NVIDIA's Vera Rubin and Blackwell GPU ramp is consuming advanced packaging capacity aggressively. Compute scarcity is structural, not cyclical.

- 🔌 **MCP hits 97M installs, 10K+ servers; Dev Summit draws 1,200 in NYC** — New AWS maintainer added; every major IDE, cloud provider, and coding platform now integrated. Builders report 40% faster multi-step workflows. The HTTPS analogy is apt — this is the standardization moment that unlocks mainstream adoption.

**Project Glasswing / Claude Mythos access tiers** — Anthropic just established a precedent: frontier models with weaponizable capabilities get restricted to vetted institutional users rather than being open-sourced or shut down. Within 30 days, watch for OpenAI and Google to announce analogous tiered access programs for their most capable cyber/offensive-adjacent models. This shapes enterprise AI procurement and compliance conversations in ways most teams haven't priced in yet.
