## Section 1: World & Market Briefing

🎯 **Iran's IRGC strikes US air base — talks are precarious, markets are rattled** — Tehran's Revolutionary Guard launched an attack on a US base it says was used in earlier American strikes, targeting Iranian drones and a launch site near the Strait of Hormuz. Trump meets Cabinet Wednesday at what's officially being called a "critical juncture" in peace talks. Oil is rising, S&P futures are falling — the Iran peace premium from earlier this week is being rapidly repriced.

📊 **PCE inflation at 3.8% annual rate — the Fed is stuck** — The Fed's preferred inflation gauge came in hotter than expected, pushing rate cut expectations further into the future. S&P 500 fell 0.23% to 7,503. With gas 51% higher since the war started, any peace deal and oil price collapse could actually do more for inflation than the Fed can right now.

❄️ **Snowflake surges 37.5% after-hours on $6B AWS deal + Q1 beat** — The cloud data warehouse announced a $6B five-year AWS commitment alongside better-than-expected Q1 earnings and revenue. Cloud spending rationalization appears to be over; data infrastructure names look good if you believe AI workloads keep compounding.

🦠 **Ebola outbreak spills from Congo into Uganda, threatening 10 countries** — Africa is racing to contain what's become a fast-spreading outbreak; hospitals have been attacked by armed men forcing evacuations under gunfire. If containment fails before it reaches a major transit hub, this becomes a global story very quickly.

🚢 **US maritime strikes in Ecuador fuel international controversy** — Ecuadorian fishermen claim a US-flagged vessel destroyed their boats at sea; the strike is one of 50+ US maritime operations across the Eastern Pacific and Caribbean that have killed nearly 200 people. Largely unreported in US media but generating significant diplomatic friction across Latin America.

🌡️ **London hits 95°F for the second straight day — government emergency warnings issued** — Two consecutive days at 95°F in London triggered official warnings. What used to be a once-in-a-generation event is becoming a near-annual occurrence, and the political cost of climate inaction is starting to show up in European polling.

## Section 2: AI & Agentic Systems Briefing

🔓 **Trump lifts AI chip export ban to China — NVIDIA and AMD back in business** — NVIDIA can now sell H200 chips and AMD can sell MI308 chips to Chinese customers; both companies have already received purchase orders. This is a massive revenue unlock for both companies and reverses one of the Biden era's signature tech-security policies. NVIDIA FY2026 revenue hit $215.9B (+65% YoY) — adding China demand back on top of that is a material catalyst.

🤝 **Microsoft-OpenAI partnership goes non-exclusive — Azure's AI moat just narrowed** — The restructured deal opens OpenAI to compete more freely across cloud providers. For enterprise architects buying Azure in part for OpenAI access, this changes the calculus. Expect other hyperscalers to move aggressively on OpenAI's new commercial freedom.

💰 **Anthropic commits $200B to cloud infrastructure with Google Cloud + Broadcom** — As revenue hits a $30B+ run rate (up from $9B at end-2025), Anthropic is locking in massive compute commitments. This is both a capacity play and a strategic hedge: the company needs guaranteed supply to serve demand that's growing faster than the industry can provision.

🔌 **MCP goes near-universal — only OpenAI Codex is holding out** — Model Context Protocol is now supported by every major coding agent: Claude Code, Google Antigravity (joined March), Cursor, Kiro, Copilot, and Windsurf. OpenAI Codex remains the lone non-adopter. **For teams building agentic platforms:** MCP composability is now table stakes; if your tool stack doesn't speak MCP, you're accumulating integration debt.

📉 **79% of enterprises claim AI agent adoption — only 11% are actually in production** — IBM Think 2026 put numbers on the hype gap. Multi-agent systems are technically demanding and vendors are slow-walking interoperability while figuring out monetization. The real question for AI delivery teams: are you in the 11%, or are you the 79% with a Slack channel and a pilot?

🔐 **OpenAI grants EU access to GPT-5.5-Cyber model** — As part of EU regulatory engagement, OpenAI is providing cybersecurity teams with early access to its specialized cyber variant of GPT-5.5. Anthropic's more capable Mythos model is reportedly still off the table for EU regulators. The AI-for-offense-vs-defense debate is going to get louder in Brussels this year.

**Sleeper Story:** The DOJ's AI Litigation Task Force — established quietly in January 2026 — has sole authority to challenge any of the 600+ state AI bills moving through legislatures as unconstitutional or federally preempted. This body will determine whether the US gets one coherent AI regulatory framework or a state-by-state compliance nightmare. It is receiving almost no coverage relative to the stakes.
